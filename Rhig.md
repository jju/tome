# Rhig Ostoroth

Rhig is an [Aarakocra](Aarakocra.md) [sage](sage.md) based in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). He has a haughty manner which doesn't match well with the company he keeps playing billiards at the [Gibbet Tavern](GibbetTavern.md) (where he's recently ([early 4996](IC4996.md)) made the acquaintance of one of [Riles'](Riles.md) alter-egos.

Most people know he was an old priest of [Ioun](Ioun.md) but he doesn't have a temple in Hammerkeep.
