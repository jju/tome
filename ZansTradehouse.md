# Zan's Tradehouse

Caravans and such going through [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) are often organized through here. The shipping concerns have representatives, but this is more for overland caravans. The [Goblin Transport Guilds](GoblinTransportGuilds.md) keep a lot of eyes in here looking for freelancers trying to avoid their services through the [Twin Woods](TwinWoods.md)
