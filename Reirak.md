# Reirak Snowcoat

Reirak was a young [Dwarf](Dwarf.md) of the [Snowcoat Clan](ClanSnowcoat.md) who died in [Memnor IC4995](IC4995.md) of a wasting disease. He was a cobbler who worked hard to support his family, always dreaming of moving to the real mountains someday. He was married to [Findex Norixius](Findex.md) and was a loving stepfather to [Kiirith Norixius](Kiirith.md).
