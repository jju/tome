Rexer the Third is a huge young purplish-pink [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) [Ranger](Ranger.md) from [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md). He has his own private island a few miles from the city, which is where his friends know to find him. He hates the idea that people hunt dragons, which are the most magnificent creatures in the world.
