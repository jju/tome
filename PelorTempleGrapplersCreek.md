# Temple of Pelor

The temple of [Pelor](Pelor.md) in [Grapplers Creek](GrapplersCreek.md) was a small wooden affair with a beautifully carved altar and benches. 

The priest, [Ignominy Persh](IgnominyPersh.md), was murdered by [invisible clawbeasts](Skulk.md) in the temple near the end of [IC4995](IC4995.md) along with the rest of the village's residents.
