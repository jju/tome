
Recently emerged from brutal civil war.

Region called Suristhanam central flood plain, fertile hills and tropical swamps.

Significant populations of [human](rules/human.md) elf [hobgoblin](hobgoblin.md) [lizardfolk](rules/lizardfolk.md) and [yuan-ti](yuan-ti.md)

Qaragarh - metropolis with teleportation circles linked to locations across the empire.

Bijabad - breadbasket region, now infested with bulettes and griffons and work crews unearth Undead. Reconstruction from the war leaves them understaffed

Churapoor - natural harbour shelters from seasonal monsoons. Big on alchemical concoctions, spices and textiles

Imperial Ulema - order of Muwahhid scholars (who worship the source of divinity rather than deities) provide spellcasting services and maintain magical streetlights and teleportation circles.
