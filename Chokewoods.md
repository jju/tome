# Chokewoods

South of the [Grimteeth](Grimteeth.md), you'd find the Chokewoods. More likely, someone will find you **in** them, and then get you snugged up nicely in a tree while they make off with all your belongings. Which is to say these woods are dangerously infested with [Bandits](Bandits.md), making taking even the well-travelled [Dwarf Road](DwarfRoad.md) through them a perilous journey.

That said, it's the main path between [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) and [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md), so many people have to brave it. And honestly, even most slow-paced cargo caravans don't usually have to overnight in the woods (and oh man do the towns on either end, [Duvenstoll](Duvenstoll.md) and  [unassigned](unassigned.md), take advantage of that).
