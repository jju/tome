# Findex Norixius

Findex is the [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) widow of the last [Warlock](Warlock.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) a [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) named [Mreksh](Mreksh.md) who was executed 20 years ago ([IC4979](IC4979.md)). 

When [Mreksh](Mreksh.md) was alive they were fairly high class mucketymucks in  [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md), but everything crashed down on them once he was exposed as a [Warlock](Warlock.md). She received little help from the [Norixius Clan](NorixiusClan.md) and moved to the [CopperHills](CopperHills.md) with her new ([Dwarfish](Dwarf.md)) husband [Reirak](Reirak.md) and son [Kiirith](Kiirith.md).

She and [Kiirith](Kiirith.md) recently moved back to [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) from a village in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md) after [Reirak](Reirak.md) died three months ago ([Memnon IC4995](IC4995.md).
