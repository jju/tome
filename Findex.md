# Findex

Findex is the [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) widow of the last [Warlock](Warlock.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) a [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) named [Mreksh](Mreksh.md) who was executed 20 years ago ([IC4979](IC4979.md)). Her son is named [Kiirith](Kiirith.md) and she recently moved back to [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) from a village in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md) after her new ([Dwarfish](Dwarf.md)) husband [Reirak](Reirak.md) died three months ago.
