A small fortified town on [FarzaBay](FarzaBay.md)

17 miles from [Saltmarsh](Saltmarsh.md)

Population: 1576

The Small Town of Seaton is almost entirely Halfling. Other common and uncommon races also live here.

Seaton has a gold limit of 800 gold pieces. No items are for sale that cost more than this amount.

Power Centers:

Conventional: City-State

Lawful Neutral

Leadership

- King: Jillian Gobblefirn, Halfling
- Majordomo: Jo Waggletop, Halfling
- Marshal: Paela Timbers, Halfling
- Chamberlain: Pearl Shadowcloak, Halfling

Community Authorities

Seaton has 15 full time guards, which is average for this size community. In an emergency, 304 adults can be conscripted, which is average for this size community.

The guard is lead by Wellby Miggledy, a level 6 Fighter.
