# Sivekkah

Population ~10000

Sivekkah is a port city in [Ikizh](Ikizh.md). It's smaller than [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) but this is where the Queens of Make-Believe reside. It's a more aristocratic place with theatres and musicians and many temples to [Corellon](Corellon.md). Also food! I would challenge you to find a better place to find a restaurant on the continent.

## Geography

Rectangularly shaped but hugging the coast, Sivekkah's harbour is protected by massive seawalls. The city itself has walls, and is patrolled by [Golems](Golem.md). These inhuman beasts are seen as an affront by many officials of the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md).

The soil is very clay-ish, which can be problematic for crops in the nearby regions.

## Demographics
The major population groups are Eladrin, Dwarves, Kobolds and Harengon. In addition to those languages, Gnomish is often heard in the street.

## Getting Elsewhere

~112 miles to [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) on the decent Tozlu road (fast - 3.5 days, but a courier can do it in 14 hours by changing horses in [Stagcross](Stagcross.md))

## Common Knowledge

There's currently a movement to create a Wizard's College (the Arcane Circle of Knowledge), but it hasn't made huge amounts of progress, at least in part because there's a lot of upset that [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) got their Bardic enterprise underway with the help of one of Sivekkah's leading magickal artists. Sivekkah's coffers are full though, so there is (at the upper social level) support for this big project.
