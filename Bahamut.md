# Bahamut, the Platinum Dragon

The crest of the Platinum Dragon is one of the most recognizable symbols in the world. Bahamut is **the** distant benevolent deity, especially revered by dragons as the first of their kind.

Holy day is called Embertide, and is a day of solemn remembrance and respect.
#### Commandments

* Stand as a paragon of honour and justice
* Smite evil wherever it is found yet show compassion to those who have strayed
* Defend the weak, bring justice to the persecuted and protect the ideals of justice and order
