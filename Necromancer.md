# The Necromancer

In the [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md) there are growing tales of a sorceror growing a dark and terrible army to sweep out across [Nuoro](Nuoro.md). Sometimes this is a threat, sometimes (especially in some [Dwarven](Dwarf.md) communities) as a promise that the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) can't last forever.
