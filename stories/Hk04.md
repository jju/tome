I can say that y'all are at an inflection point where you're very in control of the kind of story we move towards. I like to think of these things branching and focusing. In a dungeon (or temple or whatever) you're in a focused part of the story where you're trying to get through a specific situation. There are more limited ways to get through a puzzle. Then we expand out and you have lots of choices for directions
