For the last few weeks, [Riles Greyfox](../Riles.md) had been deep in disguise in getting information about the [Swirly Orb](../SwirlyOrb.md) [the Party](../TheParty.md) had brought up from the [Shadow Mirror Shrine](../ShadowMirrorShrine.md). Using his persona of Jimmy Snapshot (complete with pancake makeup covering his facial hair which caused most people in town to assume Jimmy had a really gross skin condition) Riles got close to local know-it-all [Rhig Ostoroth](../Rhig.md). He learned the orb is a valuable old relic used for scrying long ago. While trying to learn more about where he could find out how to use the thing, he got a lead on a temple to a god named [Raei](../Raei.md)[^1] out in the [Copper Hills](../CopperHills.md) which might have had a library with more/better information[^2]. 

[^1]: They spent a long time working out the pronunciation of this, which was made more difficult since Rhig is an [Aarakocra](../Aarakocra.md) without lips.
[^2]: Rhig himself was actually pretty reticent about the temple, for what it's worth.

By the time [Riles](../Riles.md) came up to check what was happening with the rest of [the Party](../TheParty.md), they'd set off for [Dennel Crossing](../DennelCrossing.md) without him. He wasn't sure whether to go after his friends or search for the temple so though he doesn’t believe in fate or gods he trusted in luck. At every decision point on his hike he flipped a coin and followed its direction. The whole way through he assumed he was on his way to the temple, until he spotted a carriage travelling his way from [an abandoned village](../Hollow.md). 

As the sun was going down, he watched as a pile of thugs and ne'erdowells emerged from the carriage and headed up to the entrance to an old mine. He approached with an offer of a two-for-one deity coupon. He tried to intimidate the halfling with his offers of holiness even speaking in the thug's native tongue, but to no use. The [Tiefling](../Tiefling.md) moved to subdue Riles the interloper and Riles started a fight. The other thugs quickly helped end it.

They tossed Riles into the mine near the back corner and set off the explosives they'd assembled inside. (Riles did manage to expend one of his bottles of holy water to extinguish the fuse once, but a [Dwarf](../Dwarf.md) came back in and relit everything (and may have gotten off a bit worse for wear in the blast).) Riles dove for a rope heading down the busted elevator shaft[^3] and shimmied his way down.

[^3]: The rope had been attached to the apparatus supporting a major freight elevator so it was secure despite the collapsing of the mine entrance caused by the explosives. Phew.

Down below [Garriff](../Barfgeld.md) and [Rurik](../Rurik.md) were assessing their injuries, taking some deep (slightly less helpful than could have been wished) breaths and trying to figure what that explosion above (that [Pog](../Pog.md) had been climbing up into) meant. It appeared the mine wasn't going to collapse, and they were in fine position to welcome back [Riles](../Riles.md). 

But what happened to [Pog](../Pog.md)?

[Riles](../Riles.md) had heard a weird "zwerm" kind of noise as he'd been descending the rope, so [the Party](../TheParty.md) decided to climb back up and see what happened to their [Gnomish](../Gnome.md) friend. While climbing they passed an opening to a level that reeked of shit and blood and death and decided that seemed like a bad place to be[^4] while they climbed higher. The next higher opening for the freight elevator opened to a narrowish passage wide enough for a person ascending to the left and descending to the right. Some debate ensued and they went stalking upwards, figuring [Pog](../Pog.md) would have been trying to escape to the mine entrance if he had fallen off the rope. [Rurik](../Rurik.md) blew his horn repeatedly to signal they were on the hunt. Though the echoing noise probably destroyed any sense of surprise they might have been maintaining, things turned out for the best when [Pog](../Pog.md) tumbled out of nowhere to join them.

[Pog](../Pog.md) reported how he'd been climbing the rope, minding his own business when the explosion came and the shaking flung him headlong into the tunnel. He'd used his expert abilities at rolling to not hit the wall, but instead found himself in a weird gray nightmare realm where they send bad gnomekids. He'd seen no stars, only a stiltwalking beast in the ashy distance before hearing [Rurik's](../Rurik.md) horn seemingly coming from a large boulder he must have tumbled through. Careful[^5] examination found a weird invisible space he could step through and he followed them up the tunnel.

[^4]: Foreshadowing!
[^5]: For Pog, at least.

After the Party got themselves sorted they realized they were quickly approaching a door. Arguments over how to approach a door that may have a burning room on the other side ensued. [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) grew annoyed and grabbed the handle[^6] and the ground fell out from under him. Quick reflexes stopped a disastrous fall and the arty argued over how to open the door that appeared to have a 5 foot invisible gap in front of it.

[^6]: For the record, it wasn't hot.

Once they figured it out [Rurik](../Rurik.md) leaned over the gap and got the door opened to find a brown-skinned, black-haired halfling in a gory apron unstrapping a bad-smelling gaunt creature from a table. She yelled at them that [Itzmin](../Itzmin.md) was supposed to give her more time, and the party tried to portray themselves as entirely sympathetic to her plight from the far side of the invisible gap.

The tense negotiation eventually led the halfling ([Teocin](../Teocin.md)) to agree to lead the party out of the mine via her escape route as long as they told [Itzmin](../Itzmin.md) that they'd killed her. And helped carry her trunk. She was carrying a bag full of paper scraps that also clanked a fair amount.

She jiggled the handle of her room in a specific manner, which restored the floor in the passageway. She (under duress) led the way down the passageway to the freight elevator and then started tapping the wall. Her confidence quickly faded, when the wall proved solid no matter where she put her hand. "It's supposed to make a 'zwerm' noise..." So [Pog](../Pog.md) mentioned he'd tumbled through that wall to a desolate wasteland then came back, which upset [Teocin](../Teocin.md) greatly. "Do you know how expensive that was!"
