For the last few weeks, [Riles Greyfox](../Riles.md) had been deep in disguise in getting information about the [Swirly Orb](../SwirlyOrb.md) [the Party](../TheParty.md) had brought up from the [ShadowMirrorShrine](../ShadowMirrorShrine.md). Using his persona of Jimmy Snapshot (complete with pancake makeup covering his facial hair which caused most people in town to assume he had a really gross skin condition) Riles got close to local knowitall [Rhig Ostoroth](../Rhig.md). He learned the thing is a valuable old relic known as a Scrying Orb and that there used to be a temple to a god named [Raei](../Raei.md)[^1] out in the [Copper Hills](../CopperHills.md) which might have had a library with more/better information[^2]. 

[^1]: They spent a long time working out the pronunciation of this, which was made more difficult since Rhig is an [Aarakocra](../Aarakocra.md) without lips.
[^2]: Rhig himself was actually pretty reticent about the temple, for what it's worth.

By the time [Riles](../Riles.md) came up to check what was happening with the rest of [the Party](../TheParty.md), they'd set off for [Dennel Crossing](../DennelCrossing.md) without him. He wasn't sure whether to go after his friends or search for the temple so though he doesn’t believe in fate or gods he trusted in luck. At every decision point on his hike he flipped a coin and followed its direction. The whole way through he assumed he was on his way to the temple, until he spotted a carriage travelling his way from an abandoned village. 

As the sun was going down, he watched as a pile of thugs and neerdowells emerged from the carriage and headed up to the entrance to an old mine. He approached with an offer of a two-for-one deity coupon. He tried to intimidate the halfling with his offers of holiness even speaking in the thug's native tongue, but to no use. The Tiefling moved to subdue Riles the interloper and Riles started a fight. The other thugs quickly helped end it.

They tossed Riles into the mine near the back corner and set off the explosives they'd assembled inside. (Riles did manage to expend one of his bottles of holy water to extinguish the fuse once, but a Dwarf came back in and relit everything (and may have gotten off a bit worse for wear in the blast).) Riles dove for a rope heading down the busted elevator shaft and shimmied his way down.
