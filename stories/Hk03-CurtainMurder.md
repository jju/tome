While [the party](../TheParty.md) headed to the [standing stones](../SkulkStones.md), [Pog](../Pog.md) slept in. Again. He headed out about town to see what was happening, found a note from his friends telling him where they were going then he saw a dog and almost got run over by a cart, which reminded him about the note, but there was another dog and some good salami, but oh, the note! Eventually he grabbed his gear and headed out of town.

Pog's meandering path took him into the [Kanliwood](../Kanliwood.md) where he saw mushrooms growing, like those kobold kids said. He didn't see any god owls before getting to [Grapplers' Creek](../GrapplersCreek.md). In the town he could see where his friends had checked out different homes and the temple and found the same gory bodies. He headed into the woods.

Only after an hour of hiking, when he reached the standing stones did Pog realize what a dangerous trip he'd just taken by himself. These woods could have been full of those [invisible slashing beasts](../Skulk.md)! Luckily none of them had attacked him on his travels. 

He could see a lot of aftermath of the party's earlier visit, including the toppled stone and a bunch of trampled snow leading off to the trees (and returning). The standing stones were obviously a larger version of gnomish welcoming stones, as one finds in every gnome settlement of any value. He decided to make a sandwich on the central stone and discovered it made a rhythmic booming noise when he did it. Some drumming experimentation followed, but he couldn't really get it to do anything other than an evenly spaced two beats.

Deep below in the [shrine](../ShadowMirrorShrine.md), [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) was taking a break from the hall of mirrors when he heard some drumming. He was weirdly at peace with it even as his analytical mind considered it could be reminding him of the drums of war.

Eventually Pog headed down the stairs, where he followed the chalk marks [Rurik](../Rurik.md) had blazed their trail with. When the passage the party had followed opened up to the right, Pog could tell that the other path had been travelled in the not too distant past.
