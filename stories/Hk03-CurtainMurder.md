While [the party](../TheParty.md) headed to the [standing stones](../SkulkStones.md), [Pog](../Pog.md) slept in. Again. He headed out about town to see what was happening, found a note from his friends telling him where they were going then he saw a dog and almost got run over by a cart, which reminded him about the note, but there was another dog and some good salami, but oh, the note! Eventually he grabbed his gear and headed out of town.

Pog's meandering path took him into the [Kanliwood](../Kanliwood.md) where he saw mushrooms growing, like those kobold kids said. He didn't see any god owls before getting to [Grapplers' Creek](../GrapplersCreek.md). In the town he could see where his friends had checked out different homes and the temple and found the same gory bodies. He headed into the woods.

Only after an hour of hiking, when he reached the standing stones did Pog realize what a dangerous trip he'd just taken by himself. These woods could have been full of those [invisible slashing beasts](../Skulk.md)! Luckily none of them had attacked him on his travels. 

He could see a lot of aftermath of the party's earlier visit, including the toppled stone and a bunch of trampled snow leading off to the trees (and returning). The standing stones were obviously a larger version of gnomish welcoming stones, as one finds in every gnome settlement of any value. He decided to make a sandwich on the central stone and discovered it made a rhythmic booming noise when he did it. Some drumming experimentation followed, but he couldn't really get it to do anything other than an evenly spaced two beats.

Deep below in the [shrine](../ShadowMirrorShrine.md), [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) was taking a break from the hall of mirrors when he heard some deep drumming in the distance. He was weirdly at peace with it even as his analytical mind considered it could be reminding him of the drums of war.

Eventually Pog headed down the stairs, where he followed the chalk marks [Rurik](../Rurik.md) had blazed their trail with. When the passage the party had followed opened up to the right, Pog could tell that the other path had been travelled in the not too distant past. He followed the chalk markings into the [temple](../ShadowMirrorShrine.md), passing by[^1] the bell hanging in the doorway.

The statues in the first room of the temple put Pog immediately at ease. He recognized these [tentacly creatures](../Cephalopoid.md) as characters in a gnomish epic wherein a kraken was flung to the top of a mountain and rescued by gnomes who shrunk the titan to manageable size, but the miniature kraken then divided and attached themselves to the gnomes' faces, controlling their bodies. Hilarity ensued. There are many volumes of these tales, colloquially known as the [Tentacle Faced Babysitters Club Saga](../TentacleFacedBabysittersClubSaga.md). Shortly thereafter Pog caught up with the rest of the party. 

After sharing their tales while they were resting from their frustrations, they headed back to a room they hadn't explored in the temple. This room had a somewhat cloudy reflecting pool in it, along with more statues, and a messily taken apart[^2] body on a table. [Riles](../Riles.md) checked the body for valuables and [Rurik](../Rurik.md) determined it wasn't killed in the same way as the Tieflings up in [Grapplers' Creek](../GrapplersCreek.md).[^3] When [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) examined the statues and masonry, he found there was carven artwork depicting figures in meditative poses on one wall. These figures were accompanied by writing in a few languages. The old Dwarvish version of the text read: 

> "Only when we reflect upon our darkest impulses does the way forward present itself."



[^1]: Without ringing it!
[^2]: Dissected? Vivisected? It was unclear.
[^3]: Possibly dragged away by an owl.
