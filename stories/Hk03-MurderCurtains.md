# A Murder of Curtains

> previously: [Hk02-MessMirrors](Hk02-MessMirrors.md)

While [the party](../TheParty.md) headed to the [standing stones](../SkulkStones.md), [Pog](../Pog.md) slept in. Again. He headed out about town to see what was happening, found a note from his friends telling him where they were going then he saw a dog and almost got run over by a cart, which reminded him about the note, but there was another dog and some good salami, but oh, the note! Eventually he grabbed his gear and headed out of [town](Hammerkeep.md).

[Pog's](../Pog.md) meandering path took him into the [Kanliwood](../Kanliwood.md) where he saw mushrooms growing, like those kobold kids said. He didn't see any god owls before getting to [Grapplers' Creek](../GrapplersCreek.md). In the town he could see where his friends had checked out different homes and the temple and found the same gory bodies. He headed into the woods.

Only after an hour of hiking, when he reached the standing stones did [Pog](../Pog.md) realize what a dangerous trip he'd just taken by himself. These woods could have been full of those [invisible slashing beasts](../Skulk.md)! Luckily none of them had attacked him on his travels. 

He could see a lot of aftermath of the party's earlier visit, including the toppled stone and a bunch of trampled snow leading off to the trees (and returning). The standing stones were obviously a larger version of gnomish welcoming stones, as one finds in every gnome settlement of any value. He decided to make a sandwich on the central stone and discovered it made a rhythmic booming noise when he did it. Some drumming experimentation followed, but he couldn't really get it to do anything other than an evenly spaced two beats.

Deep below in the [shrine](../ShadowMirrorShrine.md), [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) was taking a break from the hall of mirrors when he heard some deep drumming in the distance. He was weirdly at peace with it even as his analytical mind considered it could be reminding him of the drums of war.

Eventually [Pog](../Pog.md) headed down the stairs, where he followed the chalk marks [Rurik](../Rurik.md) had blazed their trail with. When the passage the party had followed opened up to the right, [Pog](../Pog.md) could tell that the other path had been travelled in the not too distant past. He followed the chalk markings into the [temple](../ShadowMirrorShrine.md), passing by[^1] the bell hanging in the doorway.

The statues in the first room of the temple put [Pog](../Pog.md) immediately at ease. He recognized these [tentacly creatures](../Cephalopoid.md) as characters in a gnomish epic wherein a kraken was flung to the top of a mountain and rescued by gnomes who shrunk the titan to manageable size, but the miniature kraken then divided and attached themselves to the gnomes' faces, controlling their bodies. Hilarity ensued. There are many volumes of these tales, colloquially known as the [Tentacle Faced Babysitters Club Saga](../TentacleFacedBabysittersClubSaga.md). Shortly thereafter [Pog](../Pog.md) caught up with the rest of the party. 

After sharing their tales while they were resting from their frustrations (along with [Greyfox's](../Riles.md) brief temple massage for [Pog](../Pog.md), which really chilled the vibes), they headed back to a room they hadn't explored in the temple. This room had a somewhat cloudy reflecting pool in it, along with more statues, and a messily taken apart[^2] body on a table. [Riles](../Riles.md) checked the body for valuables and [Rurik](../Rurik.md) determined it wasn't killed in the same way as the Tieflings up in [Grapplers' Creek](../GrapplersCreek.md).[^3] When [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) examined the statues and masonry, he found there was carven artwork depicting figures in meditative poses on one wall. These figures were accompanied by writing in a few languages. The old Dwarvish version of the text read: 

> "Only when we reflect upon our darkest impulses does the way forward present itself."

When [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) read that, he decided to try following the instructions. He shared "his darkest impulse" that once he had felt motivated to flee from a battle. After he said that though, he reconsidered. This was a place for true reflection; he should think and share more deeply, because this was a safe place for doing that. So in a low voice he told his companions of a task he'd had in the war, where villagers had been giving aid to the enemy. He'd spotted an insignia of an [Imperial](DantagEmpire.md) soldier hiding in a dwarf home and flew into a rage, attacking the hidden figure bearing it. When he looked at the corpse that fell to the ground he realized he'd been mistaken; that was no Imperial marker, just a scrap of meaningless cloth.

The group was silent. "So you murdered a curtain..." said [Pog](../Pog.md), with deep empathy.

Though nothing else happened when [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) told his story, the party quickly regrouped, focusing on the word "reflect" in the carving and headed for the hall of mirrors. While [Pog](../Pog.md) held hands with [Rurik](../Rurik.md) and [Riles](../Riles.md), [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) told the story again, facing the central mirror in the room. As he finished reflecting on his darkest impulse, a door swung open at one end of the room and the group was attacked by four [living shadows](../Shadow.md).

[Rurik](../Rurik.md) was attacked first and found he couldn't grapple the thing as it weakened him. These were not the same [invisible beasts](../Skulk.md) from the farm. The others tried to hack at the creatures, but the tide turned as [Riles](../Riles.md) cast a light spell on the central mirror, dazzling everyone, but sending the shadows into a full retreat. Everyone got some hits in on one of the things and [Rurik](../Rurik.md) finished it off with a Starry Wisp spell. Two of the creatures escaped out into the shrine and one escaped through the secret door that had opened.

Beyond the secret door was another chamber, the shrine proper. Four tentacle-faced statues stood across from each other in the center, while a rounded alcove held an altar topped with a [swirling crystal ball](../SwirlyOrb.md). The statues and floor (and the entire room, really) were unnaturally clean. In here, [Rurik](Rurik.md) fell back into a more contemplative state.

That's when [the ghost](../SnowcoatGhost.md) showed up.

The apparition appeared to be a youngish female [Dwarf](../Dwarf.md), that manifested from the darkness. The party tried talking to her, [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) on lead. He soon discovered her clan name was [Snowcoat](../SnowcoatClan.md) and her accent was from [Zhaalum](../Zhaalum.md) like [his](../Barfgeld.md).

At first [Snowcoat](SnowcoatGhost.md) wished only for the party to sit with her and think. [Rurik](../Rurik.md) was all in for this. She could not tell the party how long she had been in the shrine, nor was she interested in going home. She complained of ["the last one"](../TheLastOne.md) who did not bring her anyone to come and sit and think with her. All it did was bring not enough people and then "cut cut cut." It was not a sit and thinker. Could the party sit and think with her? Or at least bring her people to sit and think with?

When [Riles](../Riles.md) grew frustrated and attempted to leave her chamber with the [swirly glowing orb](../SwirlyOrb.md) she got very agitated, saying they were even worse than ["the last one."](../TheLastOne.md) A [Shadow](../Shadow.md) attacked and she unleashed her own attacks directly upon [Riles'](../Riles.md) brain, nearly killing him. In the ensuing melee she screamed horrifically at the rest of the group causing damage throughout the party.

By the time [Barfgeld](../Barfgeld.md) had connected with his axe a few times, and [Riles](../Riles.md) was revived and [Rurik](../Rurik.md) had thrown all the starry wisps at her he could, Snowcoat swirled around in her shadowy form and disappeared.

The party escaped the temple, heading up towards the surface where they were going to plan their next steps. As they came out from the stairway under the toppled stone, however, they were accosted by a large figure.

"Well well well. What do we have here?"

[^1]: Without ringing it!
