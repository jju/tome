Most Goblins in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) live in either the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) or [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md) and they get spittingly mad about the others. They have been driving a lot of the enmity between the two sides of the river despite being a relative minority as far as settled folk in the twinwoods go.
