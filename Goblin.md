# Goblins

Imperial sages[^1] describe goblins as follows:

> Goblins are small, squat creatures about 3 to 4 feet tall with pointed ears and equally pointy teeth. They have jet black hair and sometimes vary through deep brown and occasionally a deep grey or silver and skin that varies from hues of orange to hues of green. They have beady black eyes and flat faces.

At more than one time in history the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) fought wars against widespread goblin menaces in [Anor](Anor.md). Their culture was seen as antithetical to any value of order. Eventually the many variations of goblin life found their way into wider acceptance.

Most Goblins in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) live in either the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) or [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md) and they get spittingly mad about the others. They have been driving a lot of the enmity between the two sides of the river despite being a relative minority as far as settled folk go.

Goblin religion centres around [Maglubiyet](Maglubiyet.md) and a lot of them believe in reincarnation, which is how some scholars explain the utter lack of self-preservation found among many.

## Goblin Names

Goblin names are not given at birth as the average goblin life expectancy is very short and they believe giving a name to be a waste. Instead, they assign them numbers until, if the goblin is lucky or resourceful enough, they reach the age of maturity (usually after 4 years). These names are given by the local Boss and named goblins gain great respect among their comrades. If a goblin does something incredibly daring (or stupid) and gains renown, they may also be given a title describing the deed.

**Male:** Gungax, Makdox, Gizil, Bilnix Rukalog, Drax, Unkgil

**Female:** Tiwin, Kawli, Wort, Ib, Drolaw, Marowa, Walow
