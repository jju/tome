# Hammerkeep
The third city of [[Rundan]]. Population ~7500. It's a gloomy and horrible place, just on the edge of the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md). The people tend to be wary of the dangers of the forest, mcsh more than the people who live in the forest.

The Keep itself is made of dark stone and the wind howls through it.

The Imperial Warden is stationed here, making sure the king's trees and wildlife aren't taken by poachers. Officially at least. The truth is that the warden's patrols follow very well-worn and predictable paths, since leaving them makes for dead rangers.
