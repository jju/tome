# Hammerkeep
Hammerkeep is the third city of [Rundan](Rundan.md) (population ~7500), and these days it's a gloomy and dour place. People here tend to be more suspicious and withdrawn than even the most out of the way villages a day's travel eastward. Don't expect hospitality, though it's usually possible for merchants to find some sort of trade.

Population ~7500. It's a gloomy and horrible place, just on the edge of the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md). The people tend to be wary of the dangers of the forest, much more than the people who live in the forest.

The Keep itself is made of dark stone and the wind howls through it.

The [Imperial Warden](WardenofHammerkeep.md) is stationed here, making sure the king's trees and wildlife aren't taken by poachers, and keeping paths safe for Empire. Officially at least. The truth is that the warden's patrols follow very well-worn and predictable paths, since leaving them makes for dead rangers.
