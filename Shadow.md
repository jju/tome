# Living Shadows

These creatures are swirling entities of darkness[^1] unleashed on the physical world. They prefer attacking from surprise in dim light or darkness and their attacks drain the strength of their targets. They are susceptible to radiant damage, but cannot be grappled.

In the [Shadow Mirror Shrine](ShadowMirrorShrine.md) four of them lurked in wait on the far side of the mirror walls. When [the party](TheParty.md) opened a secret door out of the room, they were released from the mirrors to attack. The party destroyed one in the mirror room, and another in the shrine proper[^2]. Two escaped.

[^1]: Note that they are not the same as the [invisible creatures](Skulk.md) that appear to have been responsible for the [Grapplers' Creek](GrapplersCreek.md) massacre.
[^2]: Annihilated in one spell shot by [Riles Greyfox](Riles.md).
