# Orcs

In the Imperial documents[^1] Orcs are described thusly:

> Orcs are, on average, tall and broad. They have gray skin, ears that are sharply pointed, and prominent lower canines that resemble small tusks.

Most Orcs in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) come from the far northwest (also the traditional [Goliath](Goliath.md) homelands), but there's a substantial group of them in [Rundan](Rundan.md) who are descendants of Orcs of the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md). They share similar mythologies, but their traditional languages diverged long ago (Imperial Orcs speak a more classical form, while Western Orcishs has much more of a Giantish feel to it.)
