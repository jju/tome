Most Orcs in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) come from the far northwest (also the traditional [Goliath](Goliath.md) homelands), but there's a substantial group of them in [Rundan](Rundan.md) who are descendants of Orcs of the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md). They share similar mythologies, but their traditional languages diverged long ago (Imperial Orcs speak a more classical form, while the Western Orcs has much more of a Giantish feel to it.)
