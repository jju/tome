Skerpa Snowcoat was a middle-aged (born [IC4724](IC4724.md)) fighter against the dragonborn invasion of [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md).

After years of fighting (and Skerpa wasn't a tremendously adept fighter) Skerpa wanted to go home. Her husband had been killed in a pointless skirmish back near [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) and she was done. Throwing her life away in the battle against the Empire seemed like her best option. 

In [IC4942](IC4942.md) she discovered the standing stones and was trounced by their guardian [woodwoad](woodwoad.md). While waiting for her fate to be decided, Skerpa was stashed beside a stone. a battle broke out between imperial forces (non-dragonborn) and the dwarves Skerpa was supposed to be supporting. After the battled moved on, the guardian had been toppled and the stone reclined, allowing the stunned dwarf to find her way down the passage.

When she stumbled through the [ShadowMirrorShrine](ShadowMirrorShrine.md), her dark desire (which unleashed the shadow demon from the mirror room) was to end the war and punish the fucking leaders who caused her husband to die. The shadow demon she revealed this to was in the service of a fiend who thought this could be a way into the base of Zhaalum. Skerpa was promised a path through the underdark to Zhaalum and revenge. She could bring a host of demons through below the mountain and destroy everything she found. Skerpa balked at that, but would lead a group of dragonborn to the city to ambush the Dwarven leaders she deemed responsible. 

Skerpa was granted a shadow demon guide who disguised her and led her team of assassins through the dark. It took weeks but she eventually reached the city and sent the assassins to do their work. The demon led Skerpa offcourse to a portal that had long been buried. The demon led her to open the portal which she started, but abandoned. This would have opened a full-on crack into the abyss that had been sealed by the dragons since time immemorial (the key to unlocking this portal rests in following the arcane path the demon had led her through) but she didn't let that happen. Her punishment was to be cursed into allip-hood.

She has seen the scourges of the abyss and how they will flood the world. She knows the veil has weakened among all the planes. She needs to impart this but can only truly convey madness. After several years of wandering she made her way back to the [ShadowMirrorShrine](ShadowMirrorShrine.md) around [IC4962](IC4962.md). She likes the calming of emotions that happens through these statues the long dead illithids created. This calming effect also brings about an impulse for deep introspection which can lead to a catatonic state of madness.

When the alhoon came up from the deeps and found the shrine Skerpa tried to impart her knowledge in exchange for people to harvest calmness from. The alhoon ("the last one") would only study study study and now[^1] it is gone. Skerpa desperately wants to impart its secret to someone. There are piles and piles of maps from a previous inhabitant in the small interior room (there's a bugbear skeleton in there as well (plus treasure?)). Skerpa no longer knows her name but desperately wants a scholar to share her knowledge with. That she's cryptic and [Undead](Undead.md) and will totally cause madness damage to anyone who attacks her could prove challenging.
