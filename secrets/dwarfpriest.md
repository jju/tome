Jennar Snowcoat was a middle-aged (born [IC4724](IC4724.md)) fighter against the dragonborn invasion of [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) .

After years of fighting (and Jennar wasn't a tremendously adept fighter) Jennar wanted to go home. Her husband had been killed in one of the recent skirmishes back near [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) and she was done. Throwing her life away in the battle against the Empire seemed like her best option. 

In [IC4942](IC4942.md) she discovered the standing stones and was trounced by their guardian. While waiting for their fate to be decided, Jennar was stashed beside a stone. a battle broke out between imperial forces (non-dragonborn) and the dwarves Jennar was supposed to be supporting. After the battled moved on, the guardian had been toppled and the stone reclined, allowing the stunned dwarf to find her way down the passage.

When she stumbled through the [ThisisFineShrine](ThisisFineShrine.md), her dark desire (which unleashed the shadow demon from the mirror room) was to end the war and punish the fucking leaders who caused her husband to die. The shadow demon she revealed this to was in the service of a fiend who thought this could be a way into the base of Zhaalum. Jennar was promised a path through the underdark to Zhaalum and revenge. She could bring a host of demons through below the mountain and destroy everything she found. Jennar balked at that, but would lead a group of dragonborn to the city to ambush the Dwarven leaders she deemed responsible. 

Jennar was granted a shadow demon guide who disguised her and led her team of assassins through the dark. It took weeks but she eventually reached the city. The demon led Jennar offcourse to a portal that had long been buried. The demon led her to open the portal which she started, but abandoned. This would have opened a full-on crack into the abyss
