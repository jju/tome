Jennar Snowcoat was a middle-aged (born [IC4724](IC4724.md)) fighter against the dragonborn invasion of [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) .

After years of fighting (and Jennar wasn't a tremendously adept fighter) Jennar wanted to go home. In [IC4942](IC4942.md) she discovered the standing stones and was trounced by their guardian. While waiting for their fate to be decided, Jennar was stashed beside a stone. a battle broke out between imperial forces (non-dragonborn) and the dwarves Jennar was supposed to be supporting.
