Jennar Snowcoat was a middle-aged (born [IC4724](IC4724.md)) fighter against the dragonborn invasion of [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) .

After years of fighting (and Jennar wasn't a tremendously adept fighter) Jennar wanted to go home. In [IC4942](IC4942.md) she discovered the standing stones and was trounced by their guardian. While waiting for their fate to be decided, Jennar was stashed beside a stone. a battle broke out between imperial forces (non-dragonborn) and the dwarves Jennar was supposed to be supporting. After the battled moved on, the guardian had been toppled and the stone reclined, allowing the stunned dwarf to find her way down the passage.

When she stumbled through the [ThisisFineShrine](ThisisFineShrine.md), her dark desire (which unleashed the shadow demon from the mirror room) was to end the war in failure. The [allip](allip.md) stalled the demon's hand from killing the little warrior, wanting secrets more than to be left alone (for even more time).

Once Jennar made it into the sanctum, she was calmed. The allip told her about a path that went through the myriad twisting passages that would get all the way back home, and if she brought the right people with her, the war could end just as she wanted.
