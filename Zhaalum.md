# Zhaalum

Until [IC4890](IC4890.md) the city [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) was named [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) by the [Dwarves](Dwarf.md) who had built it over a thousand years ago in the ruins of an ancient dragon hoard[^1]. The city is built into the mountain with an ingenious system of ventilation and waste removal that is the pinnacle of any (mostly non-magical) engineering feat on [Nuoro](Nuoro.md).

[^1]: Historians differ on whether the first dwarves slew the dragon or it was already gone when they took over the mountain.

The [Dwarf Road](DwarfRoad.md) is newer than Zhaalum, but its connection to [Ikizh](Ikizh.md) is what made [Sivekkah](Sivekkah.md) and [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) into actually prosperous cities.
