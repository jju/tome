# Cave Bird Bones

In [IC4996](IC4996.md) [the Party](TheParty.md) encountered [Undead](Undead.md) animated beastly giant birdish skeletons deep in the [Hollow](Hollow.md) mine. The creatures stood upright and had vicious teeth and claws. One leapt and tackled its prey for easier biting.
