# Temple of Gruumsh

Carved from huge timbers down by the docks, this is often where travellers through [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) stop for blessings on their way upstream. Usually that's because they've heard it's a good idea from the travellers coming downstream through the [Woods](TwinWoods.md). The main priest is a dour [Orc](Orc.md) named [Simple Rhela](SimpleRhela.md).
