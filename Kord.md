# Kord, the Storm Lord
The lord of thunder revels in tests of strength and blesses those who prove their worth on the battlefield. Those who wish for clearer skies worship him to appease him.

Usually depicted as **the** warrior, often naked and masculine, wrestling a beast or otherwise displaying dominance.

The Day of Challenging is Kord's holy day often celebrated by a gladiatorial Godsbrawl.

#### Commandments

* Bravery above all. There is no glory in cowardice.
* Strength is the path to greatness, but greatness is the responsible use of strength.
* The glory of the Storm Lord lives through your own glory on the battlefield.
