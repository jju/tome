# Kiirith

Kiirith is the teenaged [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) son of the last [Warlock](Warlock.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). His father, a [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) named [Mreksh](Mreksh.md), was executed when Kiirith was just a baby ([IC4979](IC4979.md)). His mother [Findex](Findex.md) recently moved back to [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) from a village in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md) after his ([Dwarfish](Dwarf.md)) stepfather [Reirak](Reirak.md) died three months ago.
