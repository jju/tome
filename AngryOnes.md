# The Angry Ones

Relying on violence to sustain their existence, angry [Sorrowsworn](Sorrowsworn.md) -- sometimes called the Angry -- grow more powerful when their foes fight back. If a creature opts not to attack, though, this sorrowsworn becomes confused, and its attacks weaken. It also has two heads, which bicker with each other incessantly.
