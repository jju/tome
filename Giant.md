# Giants

Have you seen a giant? You look way up. Way past that [Goliath](Goliath.md) you call "Stretch." They're really really big. And according to the [Empire](DantagEmpire.md) they're always hungry and are generally a menace. Luckily they stay out of the way of people in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) for the most part.

One (sort of) reputable source says that long ago Nuoro was filled with Giants. There were whole shwacks of them all over the place. They built things and farmed creatures[^1] and generally lived their lives here until something happened and they got much rarer.

The Goliaths in the northwest tell stories of the many kinds of giants and that the ones you see down near [Rundan](Rundan.md) and [Ikizh](Ikizh.md) are mostly thought to be mere Hill Giants. There are supposed to be scarier ones out there, who can control elemental forces and generally terrorize anyone who ventures into their domains.

[^1]: It's unclear if those creatures were also gigantic, or if they're the ancestors of the fewer giant beasts we see nowadays, or if regular beasts have just gotten smaller.
