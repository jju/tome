# Quajath, the Undermaw
Creation of or progeny of Torog, but it's a deranged gargantuan worm, entombed in the frozen north. Some wildfolk have found a Tomb of the Worm and feast on its flesh making them followers who want to free it.

Monstrous worm covered in toothy scales. Jagged mouth with a ring of pointed teeth, a thrice-forked tongue bearing three ivory eyes.
