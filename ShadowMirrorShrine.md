# The Shrine with the Shadowy Mirrors

A half hour descent into the darkness from a set of [standing stones](SkulkStones.md) near [Grapplers' Creek](GrapplersCreek.md) there is a strange little shrine down a passage beyond an archway.

Inside the archway is an obviously constructed place. The walls are mortared stone with 12-foot high ceilings. The first room from this direction is circular with statues of three [tentacle-faced humanoids](Cephalopoid.md) lifting their arms upward. There are also mirrors on the walls in oddly asymmetric places.

Some people find the mirrors in this shrine unsettling, saying "the shadows move before the figure" or "there's a weird lag" or "something distorted follows me." Some people don't experience these effects.

Beyond the circular room are a couple of passages with doors made of strange mushroom planks reinforced with metal, causing them to be denser than usual wood yet oddly spongy. There is glowing moss providing a bare minimum of light throughout, and no sconces for torches (though a couple of flickering rocks give off an arcane green bit of illumination. The floors are clean despite the rubble closing off some passageways.

There is a small room with a bunk and fungiwood table and some space for food and drink. The chamberpot is long dried out.

In an intersection room there's a full-size mirror directly behind a door. It is host to more distortion effects for those susceptible and is remarkably solid.

The hall of mirrors amplifies to a dazzling degree any light source brought in. The walls and ceiling are all mirrored and the floor is a bright white stone. There's a a large double-sided mirror in the centre of the room that people can walk around. These mirrors reflect a malevolent sinister version of the subject.
