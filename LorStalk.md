# Lor Stalk - Adviser

Lor is a [hobgoblin](rules/hobgoblin.md) [Cleric](Cleric.md) of [Ioun](Ioun.md) who acts as an advisor to [Lady Hammerkeep](BidrithCrecur.md).

Lor has a prominent widow’s peak, his dark hair shot through with gray. The high steward wears plain clothing that is obviously well made. Though he is otherwise alert, the pinched look of his face marks him as one bearing some sort of strain or fatigue.
