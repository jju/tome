# Rodney's Hamlet

> Those few folk who’ve heard of it at all know “Rodham” is the abbreviated name for Rodney’s Hamlet (also known to its inhabitants as Rod’s Wood). Situated in deep forest some 25 miles west of [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md), Rodham is a remote, intentionally isolated forest commune of about nine square miles, founded during the [Grim War](GrimWar.md) as a refuge and almost entirely unknown to the wider world. Eighty people (15 families) call it home.

In the summer of [IC4995](IC4995.md) there were a pile of requests from Rodham looking for help with defeating an evil [Druid](Druid.md) that was causing wild beasts to attack the villagers. The [Warden of Hammerkeep](WardenofHammerkeep.md) did not send anyone out (most likely because the Warden is a "doughy little coward" according to local hotheads).
