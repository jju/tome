# Tiamat

**The** evil dragon god with five heads whose domain is Order, Trickery, and War. She is almost always depicted in conflict with [Bahamut](Bahamut.md), the platinum dragon god. Her symbol is a taloned dragon claw (which is pretty common in [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) societies, so it can blend in without a huge amount of overt notice).

Like all deities, she is bound not to directly interfere in the realm of mortals, able only to channel a portion of her divine energy through her unholy magic users.

#### Commandments

* Amass wealth but spend little.
* Do not forgive or forget any indignity you suffer. Punish all affronts.
* Take what you want.
