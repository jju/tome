# Tiamat

**The** evil dragon god whose domain is Order, Trickery, and War. Her symbol is a taloned dragon claw (which is pretty common in [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) societies, so it can blend in without a huge amount of overt notice).

Like all deities, she is bound not to directly interfere in the realm of mortals, able only to channel a portion of her divine energy through her unholy magic users.
