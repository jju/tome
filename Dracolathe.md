# Dracolathe

Dracolathe is the mighty city of Dragons. Once the site of an ancient hoard (owned by the legendary [Miergag](Miergag.md)), Dwarves took it over (by slaying the dragon) and built a city (called [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) using the wealth they recovered there). Then only a short century ago, the Dragonborn engaged in a [bloody war](GrimWar.md) to claim it[^1].

The symbol of the modern city is the ancient Golden Dragon named [Cyzzarun](Cyzzarun.md) that circles above it. It's more cosmopolitan than it was in Dwarven times, but the power structure is predominantly [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md). The settled [Goliath](Goliath.md) minority and the [Dwarfish](Dwarf.md) remnant are the other main power blocs.

A famine is currently hitting Dracolathe which is experimenting with a democratic form government. The council is attempting to improve condition for its citizens by turning to previously discounted forms of knowledge, but so far there haven't been huge breakthroughs. If the empire knew they would not be happy they gave away the City.
