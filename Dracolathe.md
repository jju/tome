Dracolathe is the mighty -- yet remote -- city of Dragons. Once the site of an ancient hoard, Dwarves took it over and built a city (called Zhaalum). Then only a short century ago, the Dragonborn engaged in a bloody war to claim it.

The symbol of the city is the ancient Golden Dragon that circles above it. It's more cosmopolitan than it was in Dwarven times, but the power structure is predominantly [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md). The settled [Goliath](Goliath.md) minority and the Dwarfish remnant are the other main power blocs.
