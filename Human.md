# Human

Humans are one of the many *many* [Humanoid](Humanoid.md) species in the world. Once, thousands of years ago they were much more common on the continent of [Anor](Anor.md) than they are now. They ruled in oppressive fashion throughout their many kingdoms. 

The Dantag rebellion was a multispecies revolution that replaced casual human supremacy with a political structure predicated of the plurality of species, and more problematically on anti-"mongrel" purity. Humans began to be seen as a muddy smear of the many distinctions that true people were, like they weren't proud enough of their bloodlines so they diluted them.

It's not to say that humans were genocidally eradicated, but, in the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md), they are a rump minority, barely thought about.[^1] In [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) in [IC4996](IC4996.md) they are about as rare as [Kenku](Kenku.md).
