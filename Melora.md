# Melora, the Wild Mother
Her realm is wherever the seas shift and the land grows wild. She is worshipped by hunters and all who revere natural wildness. Her mythical lover is [Erathis](Erathis.md) who balances her wildness.

She is usually depicted as a beautiful woman with green skin swallowed by a tangled wreath of hair leaves and vines.

Melora's holy day is Wild's Grandeur celebrating the vernal equinox. Sailors often sail for no purpose on this day, and land-dwellers make offerings at altars of twisted roots and grasses.
#### Commandments

* Protect the untamed wilderness from exploitation and destruction.
* Slay abominations and other mockeries of nature.
* Embrace and respect the savage nature of the world. Exist in harmony with it.
