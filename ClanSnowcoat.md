# Clan Snowcoat

A [Dwarf](Dwarf.md) clan name common in [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md). They traditionally maintained mines in the high [Grimteeth](Grimteeth.md).

The [apparition](SnowcoatGhost.md) in the [Shadow Mirror Shrine](ShadowMirrorShrine.md) claimed to be of this clan in [IC4996](IC4996.md).
