# Dalrig Gravelmantle - Pastor of Erathis

[Dwarf](Dwarf.md) [Leader](guard.md#Guard%20Captain) of the [Erathis](Erathis.md) temple in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). She has a military background and openly disdains the [Warden of Hammerkeep](WardenofHammerkeep.md). She sees [Brecken Dirtbone](BreckenDirtbone.md) as a rival, but doesn't much care for them, figuring that silent competence will out. Usually over the past decades that has been the case, especially when it comes to welcoming travelling Imperial Paladins.
