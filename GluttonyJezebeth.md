# Gluttony Jezebeth

[Tiefling](Tiefling.md) drug dispenser in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). In [IC4996](IC4996.md) she sees herself as as helping with the chilling illness by distributing substances that relieve the pain.

When [Rurik](Rurik.md) tracked her down she gave him a story about picking up some "good pinks" out in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md) a month or so ago and [JP Ratquiat](JPRatquiat.md) had tagged along (which was a terrible idea). Things had gone fine but then they were attacked by a smelly winged thing (that "sure, it could have been an [owl](GodOwls.md)") and she bolted. When JP eventually showed up back in town, not only in his useless addict state, but obviously really sick, she felt bad and has been keeping an eye on him since.
