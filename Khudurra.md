# Khudurra

Population ~15000

The larger and more outward-facing of [Ikizh's](Ikizh.md) metropoli, Khudurra is on the [DwarfRoad](DwarfRoad.md) to [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) but was spared a lot of the fighting in the [Grim War](GrimWar.md). It's the connection point between [Ikizh](Ikizh.md) and other continents so you hear a lot of languages there.

There is a movement in the city to create a proper Bardic College of Knowledge Arts and Arcana. (Their rivals call them the chickens because they do refer to it as BCKAaA).

There's a decent trade in alchemical works and potions which take place in the Styes district (a very rundown and oft-flooded part of town).

~112 miles to [Sivekkah](Sivekkah.md) on the decent Tozlu road (fast - 3.5 days, but a courier can do it in 14 hours by changing horses in [Stagcross](Stagcross.md))
