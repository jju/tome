# Bedwistre

The capital of [Rundan](Rundan.md). It's kind of a shithole, but it has a lot (I mean a **lot**) of temples. Population ~20,000 making it the biggest city in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md).

There is so much crime in this place to go with the temples. Think about a place filled with mud and the blood of people whose deals have gone south. They're finding bodies all over the place all the time. It's a good thing the clerics  hanging around all the temples *are* around to keep the plague outbreaks under control.
