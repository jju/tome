# Creed Itzmin

A spiteful [Tiefling](Tiefling.md) in his late forties, Creed Itzmin is a politician and part of the [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) council of advisors to [Bidrith Crecur](BidrithCrecur.md), where he serves as Councilor of Arms.

In [IC4996](IC4996.md) [TheParty](TheParty.md) found him at the [Hollow Mine](Hollow.md) where he was planning to raze the town, which he owned, to "prevent the spread of the disease sweeping through [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md)." He agreed to [let the party down into the mine](/Hk05.md) to explore for half of whatever they found. He expected them to meet up with him at his silverworks in town.
