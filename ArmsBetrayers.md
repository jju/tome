# Arms of the Betrayers
A sentient weapon created by the betrayer gods using the life force of greater fiends, then scattered. When one is found it grants the wielder power and wisdom of the fiend within.

* Blade of Broken Mirrors - Stone Dagger of [Tharizdun](Tharizdun.md) warps reality and has a glabrezu named Ragazuu
* Grovelthrash - Obsidian warhammer [Torog](Torog.md) can raze whole structures with one strike. lifeforce of an ultroloth named Ciria
