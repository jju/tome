# Chult

A tropical jungly island somewhere south of [Nuoro](Nuoro.md). The main settlement is Port Nyanzaru. Other places include:

* Port Nyanzaru - Eku (guide), Salida (guide), Grandfather Zitembe
* Heart of Ubtao - Valindra Shadowmantle
* Orolunga - Saja N'baza, 
* Kir Sabal - [Aarakocra](Aarakocra.md) remote monastery
* Nangalore - Zalkore
* Wyrmheart Mine - Tzindelor ([reddragon](reddragon.md))
* Dungrunglung - Krr'ook
* Firefinger - Nephyr
* Mbala - Nanny Pu'pu 
* Tomb of the Nine Gods - Acererak near Yuan-Ti temple
* Hrakhamar - dwarf forge
* Jahaka Anchorage - pirates
* Kitcher's Inlet
* Fort Belurian
* River Olung
* River Tiryki
* Omu (ruined city)

Chult is hot humid rainy, most people live in Port Nyanzaru.

Chultans ([Human](Human.md)), [Aarakocra](Aarakocra.md), Baitiri ([Goblin](Goblin.md)), [Dwarf](Dwarf.md), Pterafolk, [Tabaxi](Tabaxi.md)

Deep jungle has a lot of undead: zombies, ghouls and skeletons

Suspected: small number of weretigers, grungs (frogfolk), and [yuan-ti](yuan-ti.md).
