# Wererats

In [IC4975](IC4975.md) a band of goblinoid wererats were known to harass the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md) under the command of a bandit known as the [Rat King](RatKing.md). They were eliminated by [Pious Guardians](PiousGuardians.md) and their mine hideout was closed down.
