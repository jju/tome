# Erathis, the Law Bearer
Erathis claims dominion over civilization. Judges and rulers pay respect at her temples.

Usually she is depicted as an expressionless hooded armoured woman sitting atop a throne of pillars.

Her holy day is Civilization's Dawn (the autumnal equinox). At those feasts one seat is always left open for the king.
#### Commandments

* Utilize the company and aid of others. The efforts of the individual pale against those of the community.
* Strive to tame the wilds and defend the points of light and order against the chaos of darkness.
* Uphold and revere the spirit of invention. Create new settlements, build where inspiration strikes, and expand the edicts of the Law Bearer.
