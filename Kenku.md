Feathered folk who resemble ravens, kenku are blessed with keen observation and supernaturally accurate memories. None of them can remember the origin of the first kenku, however, and they often joke that there are as many kenku origin stories as there are kenku. Some of them paint their genesis as a curse, being a flightless bird people doomed to mimic other people’s creations. 

> Haunted by an ancient crime that robbed them of their wings, the flightless kenku wander the world as vagabonds and burglars who live at the edge of human society. Kenku suffer from a sinister reputation that is not wholly unearned, but they can prove to be valuable allies.

Other kenku recite cryptic but beautiful poems about their advent being a blessed event in which they were sent into the multiverse to observe and catalog its many wonders.

Whatever their true origin, kenku are most often found in the Shadowfell and the Material Plane, and they tend to have the coloration typical of ravens.

## Flightless Stereotypes

Although they may have evolved from avians, kenku have no wings or capacity for flight. Instead, they have arms in place of wings and bird-like talons for hands and feet. They retain bird-like features, however, with black and beady eyes and a long dark-hued beak. Head and torso are covered with soft feathers, a dark russet-brown color, while their scrawny limbs are bare and scaled like a bird's. They most resembled ravens.

Kenku tend to be slightly smaller than the average [Human](Human.md), standing at around 5 feet (1.5 meters). Because they have partially hollow bones, they are lighter than most creatures their height, averaging only 75 pounds (34 kilograms). They're generally not as strong, but they more agile and dexterous.

They typically wore nondescript brown robes, under which they concealed tools and weaponry.

## Secretive and Selfish

Kenku were by habit secretive, self-seeking, and cunning creatures. They tended to adopt [neutral](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Neutral "Neutral") or [unaligned](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Unaligned "Unaligned") views and behaviors; the majority were [evil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Neutral_evil "Neutral evil"), but [true neutral](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/True_neutral "True neutral") and [good](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Neutral_good "Neutral good") kenku could be found among them.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3) They were opportunistic and unscrupulous, not stopping at illegal or immoral acts.[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Although they were selfish, kenku actually operated surprisingly well together and with allies. A kenku was most effective when aided or aiding an ally.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

## Combat

A kenku fought well alongside allies, and not just with others of its kind. They were most dangerous when aided or when flanking a foe with an ally. Kenku sneaks and assassins backstabbed their victims this way.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Kenku could be vicious in a fight but were cowardly by nature. They would usually flee the moment a battle turned in their opponent's favor, and even surrender if it meant staying alive.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Kenku often used [saps](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sap "Sap"), [short swords](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Short_sword "Short sword"), or [shortbows](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shortbow "Shortbow") in combat, others wielded [clubs](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Club "Club"), [daggers](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dagger "Dagger"), [slings](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sling "Sling"), and even [spiked chains](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Spiked_chain "Spiked chain"). But even when disarmed, they would also slash opponents with their claws. Kenku typically wore [leather armor](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Leather_armor "Leather armor"), though a warrior might choose a [chain shirt](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chain_shirt "Chain shirt") or [buckler](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Buckler "Buckler").[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

### Skills[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku?veaction=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Skills")

A kenku could accurately mimic all kinds of noises, voices, and accents. A gifted kenku mimic could fool a foe into believing they had heard a voice they were well familiar with.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Kenku had an innate skill in the arts of stealth.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)

## Society

Kenku lived in close-knit gangs, family groups, or clans, commonly called "flocks". These flocks were distrusting of outsiders, even of other kenku.[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3) They established aeries or nests, usually hidden and often near large cities or at least in civilized lands.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3) Kenku had their own language, but typically spoke [Common](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Common "Common").[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

After hatching from their eggs, kenku remained in their home-nests with their families. Once they reached adulthood at age nine, they left their nests forever. Unwilling to travel alone, most soon banded together with other young adult kenku of similar habits for mutual protection and profit, forming new flocks. A few made their way on their own or formed partnerships with other creatures who needed their services.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)

These kenku gangs made their homes in the cities, where they loitered in dark alleys or roamed the streets together. Thanks to their skills and habits and lack of scruples, they flourished in the criminal underbelly, hatching schemes to get rich and strong. They could operate all kinds of illicit enterprises, but preferred confidence tricks and scheming. Violence was rarely their first choice. They hoarded their stolen, shiny [gold](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gold "Gold").[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Kenku, in gangs or alone, were usually hired as thieves or thugs, even as assassins and spies.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)

Typical kenku ruffians were unsophisticated [warriors](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Warrior "Warrior") who depended on numbers to overwhelm a foe.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3) A true kenku warrior cultivated a martial art in which they flitted here and there, apparently at random, but striking quickly as they did. They also made brutal attacks against those they had the advantage over.[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Serving as assassins and spies, kenku sneaks were often [rogues](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Rogue "Rogue") who especially depended on stealth and cooperation, and on acting first. Some could disappear amongst their fellows, before they would resurface and strike again. Some dedicated themselves to the path of [assassin](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Assassin "Assassin").[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Leading them on heists and raids, the ringleader of a kenku gang was a capable brawler who could urge his kind to press an attack, to move and scatter, or flee when a fight went against them.[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Kenku had a [magical](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Magic "Magic") tradition employing air magic and glowing feathers of force to hinder and harm. Unlike other kenku, they could even fly for short time, with their arts. They preferred the safety of height.[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3) It was rare to spot a kenku [cleric](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cleric "Cleric"), but the few among them usually follows gods of secrecy.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)

Some kenku dedicated themselves to the path of [assassin](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Assassin "Assassin").[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4) The kenku assassin combined the warrior's martial art and the wing mage's feathers, together with a poisoned blade.[[3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM2|152-3)

Some kenku served the [demon lord](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_lord "Demon lord") [Pazuzu](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Pazuzu "Pazuzu"), patron of evil flying things.[[6]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FC1|78-8)

## History in the Forgotten Realms

Kenku evolved originally from avians, though they lost their wings and with it their ability to fly.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4) They were said to be creations of the avian [creator race](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Creator_race "Creator race") known as the [aearee](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Aearee "Aearee").[[7]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-9)

Kenku who traced their ancestry back to [Kara-Tur](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kara-Tur "Kara-Tur") settled in [southeast Faerûn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Southeast_Faer%C3%BBn "Southeast Faerûn"), in [Estagund](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Estagund "Estagund") and the monster-overrun Beastlands of [Veldorn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Veldorn "Veldorn"), by the 14th century [DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/DR "DR").[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[8]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p96-10)

With the devastation and chaos wrought by the [Spellplague](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Spellplague "Spellplague") after [1385 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1385_DR "1385 DR"), the Beastlands spread further across the southeast. The kenku took advantage, gaining notoriety and integrating themselves in every monster-ruled city in the Beastlands, as well as in Estagund and [Durpar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Durpar "Durpar"), by the late 15th century.[[8]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p96-10)[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p87-11)

### Kenku Lands[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku?veaction=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Kenku Lands")

In the 14th century [DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/DR "DR"), kenku could be found lurking in cities throughout southern [Faerûn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Faer%C3%BBn "Faerûn"). A large but loosely connected guild of kenku thieves and assassins was based in [Zazesspur](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Zazesspur "Zazesspur"), [Tethyr](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tethyr "Tethyr"), and was called the Daggertalons. Kenku also inhabited Estagund and Veldorn in the [Shining Lands](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shining_Lands "Shining Lands") of the southeast.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-MM3|86-4)[[note 3]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-12)

By [1479 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1479_DR "1479 DR"), kenku were well-established and populous in the southeast. They were particularly prominent in the cities of the Beastlands,[[8]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p96-10)[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p87-11) and Estagund.[[10]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p128-13) In [Tirumala](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tirumala "Tirumala"), both kenku slaves and a kenku spy network served the [rakshasa](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Rakshasa "Rakshasa") rulers.[[8]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p96-10)[[11]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRPG-p94-14) In neighboring Durpar, they could be found in [Assur](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Assur "Assur"), [Vaelan](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Vaelan "Vaelan"), and other locales still held by [humans](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Human "Human"),[[12]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p118-15) and made up a significant proportion of the population. Kenku and [halfling](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Halfling "Halfling") gangs fought for control of the streets of Vaelan.[[11]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRPG-p94-14)

The kenku even established their own domain in the so-called [Blackfeather Barrens](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Blackfeather_Barrens "Blackfeather Barrens"), the badlands between the monster-ruled realms of the Beastlands. From their hidden rookery, [Blackfeather](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Blackfeather "Blackfeather"), it was rumored they commanded a secret organization of kenku loyal solely to their own kind, not to the array of different causes kenku served in the surrounding cities. More obviously, those of Blackfeather attacked all trespassers without mercy, making it a place to be feared by all.[[8]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kenku#cite_note-FRCG-p96-10)
