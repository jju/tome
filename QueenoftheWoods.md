# Queen of the Woods

The ruler of all the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) and [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md), the Queen demands tribute of all who stray from the well-worn path along the [Nuoro River](NuoroRiver.md). This has been tricky in recent times[^1] as there is a low intensity war going on between the two sides of the river.

[^1]: The conflict began in [IC4978](IC4978.md).

The Queen dwells in [Padserae](Padserae.md), a hidden community deep in the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md). Her grip on the the [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md) is much more tenuous and there are growing rumours of a [Necromancer](Necromancer.md) growing an army of dark sorcery in its western reaches.
