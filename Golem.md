# Golems

Magically created servitors (sometimes categorized as [Constructs](Construct.md)), Golems can be made of many materials and powered by magic. The [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) does not approve of such creatures (mostly because none of their gods make such things[^1]), but they are well-known in [Sivekkah](Sivekkah.md).

[^1]: Not entirely true. There are stories about [Moradin](Moradin.md) and their priests.
