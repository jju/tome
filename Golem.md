# Golems

Magically created servitors (categorized in the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) as [Constructs](Construct.md)), Golems can be made of many materials and powered by magic. 

The [Empire](DantagEmpire.md) does not approve of such creatures (mostly because none of their gods make such things[^1]), but they are well-known in [Sivekkah](Sivekkah.md), where they form a kind of city guard.

[^1]: Not entirely true. There are stories about [Moradin](Moradin.md) and their priests.
