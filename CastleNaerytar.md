Hidden deep in the [Oddlewett](Oddlewett.md), this castle was created long ago by a wizard, abandoned when the area became unbearably swampy (the immediate area is known as the Mere of Dead Men), then taken over by a group of astrologers (the Academy of Stargazers) who vanished mysteriously, currently in use by the [Wyrmshood](Wyrmshood.md).
