# The Dwarf Road

## Landmark

Built hundreds of years ago the road connects [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) with the southern coastal cities of [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) and [Siabsungkoh](Siabsungkoh.md). The tolls on materials coming down the mountain were intense, but have become even more so as the dwarves seek reparations from the [Grim War](GrimWar.md).
