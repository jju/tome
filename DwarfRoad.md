# The Dwarf Road
Built hundreds of years ago the road connects [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) (then [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md)) with the southern coastal cities of [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) and [Siabsungkoh](Siabsungkoh.md). The tolls on materials coming down the mountain were intense, but have become even more so as the dwarves seek reparations from the [Grim War](GrimWar.md).

It used to be patrolled and one of the safest (and fastest) means of travel into the [Grimteeth](Grimteeth.md) range, but now [Bandits](Bandits.md) roam, especially in the [Chokewoods](Chokewoods.md) where even dwarven engineering requires upkeep to fight the green entropy of life.
