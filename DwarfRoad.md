# The Dwarf Road

## Landmark

Built hundreds of years ago the road connects Dracolathe with the southern coastal cities of Khadurra and Siopanthan. The tolls on materials coming down the mountain were intense, but have become even more so as the dwarves seek reparations from the GrimWar.
