# The Arcane Purge
At this late date, and from this distant vantage, it's very unclear how the Great Betrayal began, but its aftermath was unmistakable even in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md). An Empire that had been sleepily accepting its wealth and favour of the gods suddenly mobilized its members to attack arcane magic users. This happened quickly through the network of temples before most people (who'd benefitted from the many uses of magic) had any idea what was going on.

The big problem of course was that the Betrayal hadn't involved many arcanists in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) so they weren't prepared to defend themselves. The purge was a huge cataclysmic event that happened far away, but trickled down (like the occasional floodings of the [Nuoro River](NuoroRiver.md), a comparison oft-poeticized) to soak everyone.
