Urdl is a female-presenting [Dwarf](Dwarf.md) with dark skin, long braids and a missing front tooth. 

She and her companion [Urdl](Urdl.md) were attacked by [Kobolds](Kobold.md) on the eastern road to [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) and were picked up by [Rexer III](RexerIII.md) and [Fillika](Fillika.md) afterwards[^1].
