Urdl is a female-presenting [Dwarf](Dwarf.md) with dark skin, long braids and a missing front tooth. She shows off that missing tooth whenever she laughs, which is often.

She and her companion [Urdl](Urdl.md) were attacked by [Kobolds](Kobold.md) on the [Blackstone Road](BlackstoneRoad.md) to [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) and were picked up by [Rexer III](RexerIII.md) and [Fillika](Fillika.md) afterwards[^1]. 

Urdl is a little annoyed because they were actually supposed to be guarding a wagon of supplies. The wagon left early(/Urdl and Green slept late) and Green said they could catch up with this shortcut. Where they were ambushed by kobolds. Urdl thinks their best course of action is to head directly to [Phandalin](Phandalin.md) and meet up with their boss Gundren Rockseeker. She can assure the party they'll be paid a gold piece per day for escorting them to meet up with her boss.
