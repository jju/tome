# Bugbears

The imperial scribes[^1] say this about bugbears:

> Neither bugs nor bears, bugbears are the hulking cousins of [Goblins](Goblin.md) and [Hobgoblins](Hobgoblin.md). They are long of limb and covered in coarse hair, with wedge-shaped ears and pointed teeth. Despite their formidable build, bugbears are quiet skulkers, thanks to a fey magic that allows them to hide in spaces seemingly too small for them.

I **do not know** why they are called bugbears. I'm sorry. I do know they are scary as fuck and you do not want to piss them off. They lurk and hide and then **boom!** you're grabbed and being strangled in the dark.

[^1]: Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:bugbear
