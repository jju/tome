On the south side of the [Chokewoods](Chokewoods.md) on the [Dwarf Road](DwarfRoad.md), this gateway town has a tower and a lot of inns for people who don't want to get beset by [Bandits](Bandits.md) on their way between [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) and [Khudurra](Khudurra.md). There is a fierce rivalry with the north side town, [Aurich](Aurich.md), which fulfills the same function 30 miles up the road.
