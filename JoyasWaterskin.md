# Joyas Waterskin

[Halfling](Halfling.md) brother to [Wenser Waterskin](WenserWaterskin.md) and [Felosial Waterskin](FelosialWaterskin.md), Joyas runs the [Lucky Strike Inn](LuckyStrikeInn.md) in [DennelCrossing](DennelCrossing.md). He has a huge belly and is easily the friendliest of the three.
