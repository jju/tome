# Old Zhaalumi

The Old Zhaalumi are partisans who've seen the actions of the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) as cruel and unnecessary, and feel the empire must be brought down (or at least removed from [Nuoro](Nuoro.md)). The flashpoint was the Empire's gifting of the Dwarven city [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) (in the [Grimteeth](Grimteeth.md)) to an Imperial [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) hero and his clan. The [Grim War](GrimWar.md) was fought to prevent the city's handover, but the brutal power of the Imperial army (supported by the Golden Dragon [Cyzzarun](Cyzzarun.md)) ensured it happened.

The war has been over for more than 50 years but most of the Old Zhaalumi are dwarves who lived there and left so these are still fresh wounds.

The group is very secretive, even in [Ikizh](Ikizh.md) and [Rundan](Rundan.md), doing their best to claim nothing so their fellow dwarves aren't implicated and persecuted. But in many parts of Nuoro, there are whispers that the bandits aren't just in it for their own greed, but are funneling weapons and resources to the resistance group (or are the resistance group themselves).
