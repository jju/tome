# Pazuzu

A feathery [Demon](Demon.md) connected to [Asmodeus](Asmodeus.md), and [Desirat](Desirat.md). [Teocin](Teocin.md) and [Creed Itzmin](Itzmin.md) are both worshippers who've benefitted from their alliance.

Pazuzu tends to "speak" in bloody eruptions (like a bird bursting from a shell), leaving her practitioners to engage in a kind of haruspicacy.
