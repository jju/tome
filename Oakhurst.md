# Oakhurst
Oakhurst is about a day's travel east of [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) along the south road. It's a village of about 900 people including the surrounding farms.
## Locations

* Village Hall - centre of government including lord mayor ([noble](noble.md)) Vurnor Leng.
* General Store - Source for supplies operated by ([noble](noble.md)) Kerowyn Hucrele.
* Shrine to [Pelor](Pelor.md) - offers advice and some healing run by ([Gnome](Gnome.md) [commoner](commoner.md)) Dem "Corkie" Nackle.
* Jail - only stone building in town. Felosial ([Halfling](Halfling.md) [guard](guard.md)) runs a force of 16 guards ([commoner](commoner.md)) and 4 [scouts](scout.md) who keep the village safe.
* Blacksmith - Rurik Lutgehr ([Dwarf](Dwarf.md) [commoner](commoner.md)) can repair arms and armour.
* Ol' Boar Inn - Owner and barkeep is a [Tiefling](Tiefling.md) ([commoner](commoner.md)) named Garon.
