# Gnome

Gnomes according to the sages[^1]:

> Gnomes average slightly over 3 feet tall and weigh 40 to 45 pounds. They are petite folk with big eyes and pointed ears, who live around 425 years. Their tan or brown faces are usually adorned with broad smiles (beneath their prodigious noses), and their bright eyes shine with excitement. Their fair hair has a tendency to stick out in every direction, as if expressing the gnome’s insatiable interest in everything around.

Most Gnomes in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) dwell in the [Perolmani](Perolmani.md) or [Far Uzak](FarUzak.md), but you can find them everywhere. Often they stick to their own small communities (where everyone has known everyone for centuries, which sounds exhausting), but they seem to have dealt well with [Imperial times](DantagEmpire.md) given the independence that's generally been assumed.

There are rumours of gnome communities that exist entirely underground. These gnomes are often seen as weird-touched and tend to be a bit more dour than your run-of-the-mill folk. They're most often discussed in taverns as "this weird little gal who hornswoggled my uncle" kinds of anecdote subjects.

[^1]: From the 2014 Legacy version of the Gnome https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/18-gnome

## Gnome Names

Gnomes love names, and most have half a dozen or so. A gnome’s mother, father, clan elder, aunts, and uncles each give the gnome a name, and various nicknames from just about everyone else might or might not stick over time. Gnome names are typically variants on the names of ancestors or distant relatives, though some are purely new inventions. When dealing with humanoids who are “stuffy” about names, a gnome learns to use no more than three names: a personal name, a clan name, and a nickname, choosing the one in each category that’s the most fun to say.
