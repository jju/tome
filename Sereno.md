# The Wasting Chills

Sereno is the name the surgeons at the [Circle of Healing Arts](CircleofHealingArts.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) have given to a deadly affliction that inflicts a supernatural chill on its victims and eventually kills them. They're unsure if it's actually an illness or some sort of magickal curse.

Folk who have fallen ill speak of those winds blowing mostly at night, and the general advice is against staying out after dark.
