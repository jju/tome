# Clan Battlehammer

A [Dwarf](Dwarf.md) clan from [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md).

[Garriff Battlehammer](Barfgeld.md) is a member of the clan, though he is currently ([IC4996](IC4996.md)) in [Rundan](Rundan.md) adventuring. His grandmother used to hang up strange wooden effigy bundles in trees to keep evil spirits away.
