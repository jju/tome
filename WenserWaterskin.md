# Wenser Waterskin

Wenser is a [Halfling](Halfling.md) apprentice blacksmith in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md), working for [Thalane Truthhauler Kalukithino](ThalaneTruthhaulerKalukithino.md). He's got dark skin and bristly black hair. He tries to grow a beard but it mostly just makes his face dirty.

Wenser is not very good at blacksmithing. You really need some upper body strength to get things done well, but he doesn't have it. It doesn't help that all of Truthhauler's tools are built [Goliath](Goliath.md)-sized. Truthhauler doesn't pay him a lot, but does do it regularly. [Xokx](Xokx.md), the shop's neighbouring blacksmith, is constantly telling Wenser he should come work for her, but he still feels he owes the big man a lot.

He has taken in [RilesGreyfox](RilesGreyfox.md) as a free lodger, at least in part because he misses his huge family over in [Oakhurst](Oakhurst.md). He's kind of aware he's being taken advantage of, but also truly believes in hospitality and being kind to wandering strangers, as that's what his family has always done. He's been taking advantage of getting some good deals for "supplies" Greyfox says the [Faceless Stranger](FacelessStranger.md) needs.
