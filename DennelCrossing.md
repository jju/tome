# Dennel Crossing

A small village in [Rundan](Rundan.md) about a half-day's walk from [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) on the main road. Its central hub for travellers is the [Lucky Strike Inn](LuckyStrikeInn.md). It has a wooden pallisade and a bridge over a tributary to the [Nuoro River](NuoroRiver.md). 

It serves people in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md) who are mostly shepherds ranchers or miners. Usually there are a couple of guards at either end of town, since there has been bandit activity in the area when the [Pious Guardian](PiousGuardians) patrols are scarce.
