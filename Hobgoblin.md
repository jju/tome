# Hobgoblins

The imperial sages describe hobgoblins like so:

> Hobgoblins are large goblinoids with dark orange or red-orange skin. Hobgoblins are generally taller than their goblin cousins but not quite as big as bugbears. They have curved, pointed ears and noses that turn bright red or blue during displays of emotion.

Often if you get lost in the woods in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) the person that might get you out of a deadly situation is going to be a hobgoblin. They tend to be very good at that kind of thing, but there will be a catch. 

They take gifts and gift-giving very **very** seriously. If you renege on a deal with a hobgoblin they will fuck you over without end.
