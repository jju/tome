# Kanliwood Patrol Group

Led by [Ned Lolvodug](NedLolvodug.md) these [Goblinoids](Goblinoid.md) were a team sent by the [Queen of the Woods](QueenoftheWoods.md) from [Padserae](Padserae.md) to patrol the southeastern parts of the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) in [IC4996](IC4996.md).

* [Ned Lolvodug](NedLolvodug.md) - [Hobgoblin](Hobgoblin.md) [Ranger Captain](rules/hobgoblin.md#Hobgoblin%20Captain)
* [Alzek the Shallow](AlzekShallow.md) - [Goblin](Goblin.md) [Scout](scout.md#Scout)
* Agruzoc the Shallow - [Hobgoblin Warrior](rules/hobgoblin.md#Hobgoblin%20Warrior) - quiet with an unresolved head injury - has a cloakclasp from the [IronWroughtFellowship](IronWroughtFellowship.md) - indifferent DC15
* Glagronorg Rush - [Hobgoblin Warrior](rules/hobgoblin.md#Hobgoblin%20Warrior) (f) - buddies with Etch - knows someone who escaped the necromancer - friendly DC15
* [Kukvanod Etch](KukvanodEtch.md) - Grouchy [Hobgoblin Warrior](rules/hobgoblin.md#Hobgoblin%20Warrior)
* Zrukvoron "Kill" - [Bugbear Stalker](rules/bugbear.md#Bugbear%20Stalker) (f) - easily annoyed which makes her stalk off - has seen the sickly owl - hostile DC15
