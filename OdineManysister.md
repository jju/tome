# Odine Manysister

A [Halfling](Halfling.md) tough in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) who tried to run [Rurik](Rurik.md) in for a fake reward over the winter and got beaten up badly for her trouble. She harbours a grudge (feeling that Stoneblood owes her a debt). She frequents the [Lodestone Tavern](LodestoneTavern.md) and [Harol Garrick](HarolGarrick.md) is kind of sick of her bullshit.
