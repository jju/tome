# Gruumsh, the Ruiner

The orderless and honourless marauder god blesses hunts to grant spoils worth ruining. Usually depicted as a hulking behemoth of an [Orc](Orc.md) with a missing eye.

Worshippers generally hold [Corellon](Corellon.md) in great antipathy (somewhere in the mythcycle Corellon shot out Gruumsh's eye) and long to slaughter the worshippers of the Arch Heart.

#### Commandments

* Ruin. Conquer. Kill.
* The weak exist to be crushed by the strong. Be the strong.
* There are no emotions but fury and joy. The rest are weakness.
