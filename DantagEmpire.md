# The Dantag Empire

The hills are large and the emperor is far away. Nowadays [Rundan](Rundan.md) is the most Imperial part of [Nuoro](Nuoro.md), with its many varied Pious Guardians who patrol the province's roads and plains.

Their biggest military hero (an [aarakocra](aarakocra.md) warlord) is based in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) but keeps drills going everywhere she has any reach.

[Bedwistre](Bedwistre.md) is a shithole kind of place, but it has temples. So many temples and their attendant bells, gongs and cheap tin whistles that make you want to stuff your ears with mud.
