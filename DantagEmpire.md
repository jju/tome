# The Dantag Empire

The hills are large and the emperor is far away. Nowadays [Rundan](Rundan.md) is the most Imperial part of [Nuoro](Nuoro.md), with its many varied Pious Guardians who patrol the province's roads and plains.

Their biggest military hero (Eqias an [aarakocra](rules/aarakocra.md) warlord) is based in [Hammersford](Hammersford.md) but keeps drills going everywhere she has any reach.

[Bedwistre](Bedwistre.md) is a shithole kind of place, but it has temples. So many temples and their attendant bells, gongs and cheap tin whistles that make you want to stuff your ears with mud.

## History

The [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) was formed almost five thousand years ago across the sea in the land of [Anor](Anor.md) from an alliance of the sapient species who shook off their domination by the smear of humanity. The (now somewhat mythical) founders were the [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md), [Tiefling](Tiefling.md) and [Aarakocra](Aarakocra.md) resisters to that previous empire, but over time they gathered others to their banner of "strength in difference." The seat of empire is in the glorious capital, [Drudensk](Drudensk.md).

The empire claims blessings from almost all the gods and believe those gods grant them their right to rule as much of the world as exists. The Empire has a lasting enmity towards the entire concept of [Undeath](Undead.md), making necromancy (even for benevolent purposes) one of the highest crimes.

There has been lasting enmity towards the "mongrel ages" and the people who forged them (most notably humans, but the species that are merely "short humans" or "large humans" or the like without showing their distinct lineage in their very bodies share some culpability). You can see this in the power structures all over, where generally the "smeary" humanoids[^1] aren't as prominent as their populations might suggest.

[^1]: Such "indistinct" species include Humans, Halflings, Dwarves, Goliaths, Half-anythings who are all seen as shameful bearers of a lack of distinction. Elves and Orcs sometimes count as distinct and sometimes not, usually depending on the point the Imperial is trying to make. _Note to self: include a section on speciesism in the Dantag Empire_.

When the Empire came to [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) their emissaries didn't come with a sword. I mean, yes they did come with swords, many swords, but they weren't there to conquer with force. As far as the records in Bedwistre show they came with a request for emissaries from all the polities that existed to come and pledge fealty to the Emperor and gain the protection of Empire. Many years passed before the grand continental council actually convened, and there were evidently years of discussions that took place but agreements were made with nearly every group (most notable exceptions being the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) [Eladrin](Eladrin.md)). These were also the meetings where the land was officially named for the [Nuoro River](NuoroRiver.md) (originally meant to be a recognition to bring the Eladrin into the fold, since that was their name for the upper reaches, but it didn't work).

In reality, most of those groups were then left to their own devices, as this was only a small part of the Empire's holdings, and they had other machinations going back on the continent. There were still local kings and queens and lords, even within Rundan; they just all had to know they bend the knee when Empire comes to town. The most influential mark of being part of the empire that you can still see today is the widespread custom of funerary cremation, and the diversity of species in roles of power.

After the Arcane Purge, Imperial influence began its decline (from not so great a height). The [Grim War](GrimWar.md) happened because of Imperial ignorance of the continent, and nowadays it's seen as mostly a joke outside of the Imperial troops and the rulers in [Rundan](Rundan.md).
