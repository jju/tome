# Druids

Most people in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) have never seen a true druid. They're weird shapeshifters in touch with primordial elemental forces that are outside of the [Empire's](DantagEmpire.md) conception of supernatural powers. They don't treat with powerful beings in the structures humans have built for such things but deal directly with the natural forces of the universe. They aren't big "people" people.

Now, some Imperial scholars see Druids as being part of the clerical power structures calling them [priests](Priest.md) "of nature" (usually folding them in as worshippers of [Melora](Melora.md) or [Naviask](Naviask.md) or [the Traveler](Traveler.md) or [Zehir](Zehir.md) or [Hiatea](Hiatea.md)). Some say they are more like [rangers](Ranger.md) who don't go hunting but sit around eating mushrooms and talking to animals. Regardless of what these scholars assert, they are mysterious folk.

That said, there appear to be a couple of them in around the [Twin Woods](TwinWoods.md). There are stories of children being saved by a bear that could speak, a village being terrorized by not-quite-wild animals, that kind of thing.
