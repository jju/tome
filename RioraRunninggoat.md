# Riora Runninggoat

Riora is a [Halfling](Halfling.md) [entertainer](entertainer.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). Usually she sings at the Gibbet, but if the Minstrel and Cask need someone she's been known to step it up a notch (those gigs never stick because she loves to make fun of people in the audience). Occasionally she goes out on tour.
