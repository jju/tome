# The Rat King

The Rat King was an old bandit named Gannu who led a gang of wererat [goblins](Goblin.md) that terrorized [Dennel Crossing](DennelCrossing.md) in [IC4975](IC4975.md)[^1]. 

The wererats' hideout was up in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md) in a mine where the purest silver practically falls out of the rock. Nobody ventures into that mine because long before that foul necromancers haunted the area creating zombies to claim the wealth, tainting anything that comes out of it with evil. Or so the story goes.

Some people in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) are saying that the Rat King was responsible for the [Grapplers' Creek](GrapplersCreek.md) massacre in late [IC4995](IC4995.md).

As of [Iall 25, 4996](IC4996.md) the mine was still boarded up after [Ifa](Ifa.md) and [Uroxan](Uroxan.md) checked on it to ensure it had nothing to do with the [wasting chills](Sereno.md) outbreak.

[^1]: Many of the town's [Halfling](Halfling.md) population believes this is bullshit and that people just call the leader of any gang of bandits the Rat King.
