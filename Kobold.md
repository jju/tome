# Kobolds

Imperial sages say this about Kobolds:

> Kobolds are small draconic humanoids who are typically timid and shy away from conflict. They are dangerous and vicious if cornered.

That is not a lot of information. In [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) there are a lot of them living in the mountains surrounding [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md). History shows that they have been there much longer than the coming of the [Imperial](DantagEmpire.md) [Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md), but most (non-Dragonborn) folk think of them as their little cousins.

One thing you might notice about them is that kobolds tend not to have very expressive faces, and really express their emotions using their tails (which Dragonborn don't have).
