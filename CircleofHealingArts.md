# The Circle of Healing Arts

The "best" source of healthcare in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) they specialize in strange illnesses. Run by two barber surgeons ([Lumom Bouldersorrow](LumomBouldersorrow.md) - Tortle & [Olzularg Grimace](OlzulargGrimace.md) - Hobgoblin) who are more academic than healing oriented. They also have one low-level cleric - [Jade Gift](JadeGift.md) - on staff who can heal 2d4+2 HP/day.

[Kiirith](Kiirith.md) has been hanging out here for weeks, drawing sick townspeople since his stepfather died of [Sereno](Sereno.md) at the end of [IC4995](IC4995.md).
