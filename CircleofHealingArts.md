The "best" source of healthcare in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) they specialize in strange illnesses. Run by two barbersurgeons ([noble](noble.md)) (Lumom Bouldersorrow - Tortle & Olzularg Grimace - Hobgoblin) who are more academic than healing oriented. Also have one low-level cleric Jade Gift - Tabaxi ([Priest Acolyte](rules/priest.md#Priest%20Acolyte)) on staff who can heal 2d4+2 HP/day.

[Kiirith](Kiirith.md) has been hanging out here for weeks, drawing sick townspeople.
