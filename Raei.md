# Raei, the Everlight

Raei spread the message of understanding and optimism in dark places. She was betrayed by the other gods (most notably [Asmodeus](Asmodeus.md), Lord of the Nine Hells), and neglected by most theologians<!-- but her followers have been a voice of reason and empathy in cynical times-->.

She is represented as a darkskinned humanoid female with light hair and bright wings.

Raei's holy day has long been forgotten and most places in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) you would be hard-pressed to find a worshipper, but it is said there used to be a temple to her in the bottom of a twisted canyon in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md).
