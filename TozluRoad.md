The road in [Ikizh](Ikizh.md) that connects [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) and [Sivekkah](Sivekkah.md) (~112 miles). The land here is wide open and the road is good, making fast travel very possible. 

There are numerous villages on the road that are basically just stables and inns for people passing through, the biggest one is [Stagcross](Stagcross.md).
