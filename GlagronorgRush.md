# Glagronorg Rush

[Hobgoblin Warrior](rules/hobgoblin.md#Hobgoblin%20Warrior) who is part of the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) [patrol](LolPatrol.md) led by [Ned Lolvodug](NedLolvodug.md). She's the friendly one who shared her weed, and is buddies with grouchy [Kukvanod Etch](KukvanodEtch.md). She knows someone who escaped the [Necromancer](KaranliwoodNecromancer) in the [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md).
