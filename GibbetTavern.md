# The Gibbet

A weirdly gentrified tavern near the centre of [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) with lots of old folk who still have their teeth. It's run by [EkemonLust](EkemonLust.md), a middle-aged (and quite dashing) Tiefling.

[Rhig Ostoroth](Rhig.md) likes to hold court at the Gibbet, where they have a very expensive billiards table (the best one in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md)).
