# Rundan
The furthest Western reach of the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) which controlled the entirety of [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) until the [Grim War](GrimWar.md). The last centuries have seen [Ikizh](Ikizh.md) claim independence, along with the [Croaklands](Croaklands.md). The [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) and [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md) might still register as part of the empire back across the sea, but local Rundan might doesn't make any such claims.
