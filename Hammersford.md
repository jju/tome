# Hammersford
The furthest Northern city of [Rundan](Rundan.md), it's very different from [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). (Also very different from the [Grimtooth](Grimteeth.md) village [Hammerford](Hammerford.md)). Population ~10,000

This is a river town where trade from the vast surrounding farmlands rules. A cosmopolitan place, this is what the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) 

The military centre of Rundan, the city is home to the [[PiousGuardians]] and their [Aarakocra](Aarakocra.md) warlord leader.
