# The Tentacle Faced Babysitters Club Saga

A [Gnomish](Gnome.md) epic wherein a kraken was flung to the top of a mountain and rescued by gnomes who shrunk the titan to manageable size, but the miniature kraken then divided and attached themselves to the gnomes' faces, controlling their bodies. Hilarity ensued. There are many volumes of these tales where [Pog](Pog.md) grew up.
