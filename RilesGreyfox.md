“Most people see me as a Paladin. I see myself as a small business owner. Hired swords are a dime a dozen and to stand out from the crowd you need a hook, a market differentiator. Small towns need protection but you know what those rubes love more than protection? Protection with a heavy dash of religion. So give the people what they want - am I right! The genius of my plan was to invent a god for my protection business. I call them the faceless stranger. It’s a great schtick - worship and fawn over the stranger who enters your town (sound like someone - wink). Hot meals, a warm bed, and the perfect cover. Now I know this is going I sound crazy though; I’m starting to hear whispers of what I should do. Where I should go. I can do things I couldn’t do before. Is it the faceless stranger?  Did I do too good of a job selling the sizzle? Can you manifest a god with only handful of idiot believers? Or have I tapped in to something older?”
