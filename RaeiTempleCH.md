# Temple of Raei

There is an old temple mound to [Raei](Raei.md) outside of [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md). It's been abandoned for decades and any trails to it are long overgrown, but at one time it had a library.
