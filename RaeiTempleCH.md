# Temple of Raei

There is an old temple mound to [Raei](Raei.md) outside of [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) somewhere in the twisty depths of the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md). It's been abandoned for decades and any trails to it are long overgrown, but at one time it had a library.
## Rumours

There've been a few travellers out through the Hills last year who claim they were attacked by bandits in the area. Some rumours claim those bandits are holed up in the old [Hollow mine](Hollow.md), some say they heard someone escaped from the bandits from a stone building with "a reeking cesspit of a pool" that could be this temple. The [Warden's](WardenofHammerkeep.md) rangers didn't find anything out there (though the Warden's patrols are seen as pretty useless in general).
