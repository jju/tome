# Ioun, the Knowing Mentor

Commonly depicted as a mature graying old woman of whatever species surrounded by books and scrolls.

Largely forgotten, she's primarily revered by the intellectual monasteries of the Cobalt Soul.

#### Commandments

* Unmask those who would destroy Ioun.
* Uphold and teach the value of reason, perception and truth in guiding one's emotions and path.
* Condemn those who lie without moral cause. Never stoop to the level of selfish liars.
