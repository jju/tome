# The Lodestone

A sunken damp Dwarf drinking hole, usually the first stop of Imperials in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). The barkeep is [Harol Garrick](HarolGarrick.md), a youngish halfling.

[The Party](TheParty.md) has met up here on numerous occasions through the winter of [IC4995](IC4995.md). There's a [Tiefling](Tiefling.md) named [Diswoff](Diswoff.md) who frequents the place that [Barfgeld](Barfgeld.md) is positive is a card cheat (though [Pog](Pog.md) has totally won some silver off of him).
