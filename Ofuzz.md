# Ofuzz - Assistant to the Warden of Hammerkeep

Ofuzz is a small female presenting [Goblin](Goblin.md) [Warrior](rules/goblin.md#Goblin%20Warrior) who works for [Suffering Yilmaz](WardenofHammerkeep.md). She has long dark hair and wears roughly beaded rocks that may or may not be valuable stones around her neck and braided in her (usually pretty tangled) hair.
