# Shrine to Moradin

A small stone shrine to [Moradin](Moradin.md) near the docks in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) maintained by the elderly [Hlin Thrahak](HlinThrahak.md), who fought in the [Grim War](GrimWar.md). She keeps a low profile, but has old military records stashed away in the ceiling.
