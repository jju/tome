# Ratfolk

> I know a scholar who finds Ratfolk exceedingly interesting and have reproduced [some of their work](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Os9b7vIina0gS9BDMTTmeyuI_VOdirRG/view) below since the Imperial sages don't seem to discuss them at all. - *hateswords*

A few Ratfolk seem to be found in every city, tucked away in the crevices of a rundown district or passing through as a group of bright eyed merchants. Small, diminutive, and often quick with a sarcastic remark, they blend into the background noise of a cityscape or small forest community. And that is exactly how they prefer to have it.

Ratfolk come in a variety of colors, coats, and builds. But even more diverse than their appearance is their curiosity. It is seldom a day that goes by that a Ratfolk isn't poking their nose into a new experience or trinket, for good or bad. It is said that a Ratfolk that sits still too long may die of boredom.

## Ratfolk Names

Ratfolk are given names at birth in Rattus, their native language. As a language of squeeks and chitters, there are no equivalent versions of these names in Common. Instead, a Ratfolk will pick a name from the surrounding culture or inevitably acquire a nickname from their acquaintances.
## The Mischiefs Abound

A common thread about Ratfolk communities is that they are seldom invited to set up shop by the locals. Most often a collection of them will simply and suddenly appear and then begin to nestle into dwellings as they please. These small self-contained communities are called mischiefs. Each
mischief is a complex web of social bonds and hierarchies which shift as new Ratfolk arrive, others leave for some reason or other, strong leaders grow feeble, or as the whims of the group change without provocation. 

Ratkin feel a strong need to be with others, either within an established mischief or with a group of close friends. A wandering Ratfolk will grow sad and long for the world if unable to find a community of some sort.

This need for companionship leads some Ratfolk struck by wanderlust to join up with groups of adventurers, with who they will forge a deep bond. While a tired idiom to many, for these Ratfolk the real treasure are the friends and stories they made along the way.

Ratfolk communities revel in variety, color, and collections. Friendly competitions to collect complete sets of things are common, as are eccentric home decorating parties. As such, most in rat folk culture tend toward chaos.

Ratfolk communities can be found in and around large urban areas. These communities tend to be eclectic and diverse, with those of rat folk ancestry welcoming others to join them with open arms. Indeed, rat folk communities collect not only members but virtually anything they can find, with members often patrolling the cities in which they live and bringing back piles of junk to repurpose. To outsiders, rat folk communities often resemble piles of trash, but rat folk tend to find the clutter comforting. And despite the refuse and garbage they collect, the rat folk community members themselves are as clean as any other citizens of the city. ## The Swarmhead's Great Horde

Spirituality is a complex subject for Ratfolk. Typically a local mischief will adopt similar faith practices to the communities they are closest to.

However, in addition to this worship each mischief also harbors belief in an entity known simply as the Swarmhead. Belief in this entity seems to inevitably and organically creep into the belief system of every Ratfolk group. Mischiefs totally isolated from each other seem to have an uncanny spiritual congruence.

Some claim it is a great cosmic rat which cares for each Ratfolk individually, waiting for the right time to lead the swarms to paradise. Others claim it to be not an individual but the collective  willpower of a rising tide of vermin which will one day engulf all that ratkind has touched. For as many mischiefs of Ratfolk exist, there are just as many beliefs about the nature, intentions, and purpose of the Swarmhead.
## Fancy Rats

Among the mischiefs which settle into the sleepy corners of cities are those Ratfolk who busy themselves with past times and small talk. Between their frequent meals, social engagements, grooming breaks, and general ruckus it is a wonder that they can somehow pull a livelihood.

The Fancy Rat's secret is to pull together for one another as needed, giving freely from one to another in the mischief and lean into the tight bonds of kinship that they have formed. This creates a complex web of favors, gratitude, and debts which are traded amongst each other as freely as coin. As long as the mischief prospers, temporary grievances and slacking can be overlooked. But in times of need, each Ratfolk holds one another accountable to put forward what is needed for the mischief, and then some.
## Pack Rats

In each mischief there will be certain individuals drawn towards a more industrious or mercantile lifestyle. Ratfolk who seem content to set up a shop of curiosities, a small work bench, or an eclectic haberdashery and earn their coin with their hands. These are the Pack Rats.

Less common is for a small mischief of Pack Rats to travel as a caravan, each laden with wares, raw materials, and crafting equipment. While rare, these Pack Rats are vital to the greater Ratfolk community as they form the social strands that connect the various mischiefs strewn about here
and there. Along with trade goods the Pack Rat brings with them the invaluable commodity of news and information.

## Sewer Rat

Teeming within the neglected sewer systems and abandoned catacombs which lay beneath overcrowded cities creeps a civilization which seldom sees the light of day. To those outside of the gang-like mischief a Sewer Rat is closer to a menace and public hazard. But for those in the mischief there is a bond of unyielding comradery and loyalty.

Against all odds the Sewer Rat mischiefs thrives on the discarded refuse of those who live on the surface above. Nothing is put to waste, each scrap of food, discarded bobble, and worn out tool cherished and put to best use. And what cannot be scavenged will be stolen without remorse, traded for with whatever unscrupulous merchant dares come near, or amicably bartered for over a communal meal if a Pack Rat merchant happens to visit.

## Wood Rat

Not all Ratfolk congregate within cities. Those who live closest to the old ways of Ratkind make burrowed conclaves within the forests and glades. Never too far from a trade route, but never too close as to bear the pollution of urbanization. They strive for a simple life consisting of communal farming, sustainable foraging, and the occasional wanderings through their neck of the woods.

A Wood Rat mischief consists of a series of simple abodes set within the earth. From the surface the community may appear to be nothing more than a collection of humble round doors nestled within the sides of hills or wooded outcroppings. But behind those doors lies a well-constructed
and often quite expansive network of well-furnished living spaces and hallways carved into the earth.
