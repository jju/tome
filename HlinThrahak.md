# Hlin Thrahak

Old dwarf [soldier](soldier.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) who fought in the [GrimWar](GrimWar.md) with [SkerpaSnowcoat](SkerpaSnowcoat.md). She keeps the Erathin shrine in the Docks, which doubles as an [OldZhaalumi](OldZhaalumi.md) archives. She has the records of who died in the area here in the war.
