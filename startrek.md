As a teenager, I loved Star Trek: The Next Generation (TNG). 

I was first introduced to it when I was in grade 3, the year my cool older cousin lived with me because she was going to the university my mom an I lived near. I couldn't tell you what episode I saw first (it would have been Season 1 or 2, so not good trek by any means), but I remember her telling me about Data and how he was kind of like a robot but wanted to be human. 

I know I didn't make the connection between Reading Rainbow Levar Burton and Geordi LaForge Levar Burton, but he quickly became my favourite character. I also remember being confused because Cheryl told me how they'd blown up the Enterprise but they were also flying around in the Enterprise. (I realize now she was telling me about the movies and what happened in The Search for Spock.) That said, I didn't really watch a lot of Trek until a few years later.

It's weird to think about the timespan TNG covered, since by the time I was 14 Star Trek was a huge part of my personality.
