# Lolth, the Spider Queen

An evil god of deceit, shadows and spiders, she can see through all the eyes of spiders, so she is truly all-knowing. These days she is worshipped less, and is said (by her priests) to be very prickly about it.

Usually Lolthite icons depict an alluring woman with dark purple skin and silver hair and an abdomen that transitions to a giant monstrous spider.

Lolth apparently holds a grudge against [Kord](Kord.md) for impaling her against a cliff. She also hates [Corellon](Corellon.md), and is bent on the destruction of the bloodless followers of [Luxon](Luxon.md).

#### Commandments

* It is better to be loved than feared, but try to be both.
* Misdirection, slander and shadowed steps have more function than direct conflict.
* Death to the Fey living under the sun.
