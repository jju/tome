# Felosial Waterskin

Felosial is a [Halfling](Halfling.md) [guard](guard.md) who runs a force of 16 guards ([commoner](commoner.md)) and 4 [scouts](scout.md) who keep the village of [Oakhurst](Oakhurst.md) safe. He is the younger brother of [WenserWaterskin](WenserWaterskin.md), a blacksmith apprentice in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) but doesn't care much for his older sibling.
