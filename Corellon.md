# Corellon, the Arch Heart

Guardian of spring, beauty and the arts, Corellon is not particularly in favour in the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) since they were also the patron deity for most arcane magic (see the [Arcane Purge](ArcanePurge.md).

Most tapestries depict Corellon as an androgynous [Eladrin](Eladrin.md) of impeccable grace and beauty.

Holy day is called elvendawn (Midsummer) and commemorates the emergence of the first Eladrin from the [Feywild](Feywild.md).

#### Commandments

* Create, inspire and find beauty in all you do.
* Follow the echoes of lost magics, forgotten sites and ancient art.
* Combat the followers of [Lolth](Lolth.md) wherever they might be.
