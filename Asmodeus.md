# Asmodeus, the Lord of the Nine Hells
Mastery of tyranny and domination, usually depicted as a monstrously handsome red-skinned humanoid with two curling horns. Evil entities pay homage to him, along with his many devils and demons and not a few [warlocks](warlockfiend.md).

His arch-nemesis is [Avandra](Avandra.md), but in the myths he betrayed and slaughtered all the followers of [Raei](Raei.md).

#### Commandments

* Assert dominance and power over others. Show your strength of will in the image of the Asmodeus.
* Repay cruelty done unto you with further evil. If shown kindness, exploit it.
* Do not pity those you climb over to ascend to power. Compassion is for the weak.
