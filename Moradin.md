# Moradin, the All-Hammer

Moradin is worshipped by smiths, artisans and miners. He shaped the mountains and is the patron protector of home and family.

Many guildhalls have images of a faceless dwarf of immense strength hunched over a flaming heart.

Moradin's holy day is Deep Solace, where devout followers spend the day in isolation meditating on how to be a better family member. There are also lots of feasts.

##### Commandments

* Remain stoic and tenacious in the face of catastrophe.
* Uphold and promote loyalty to yor family, your clan and your people.
* Legacy is paramouny. To create something that lasts is to change the world for the better.
