# Lake Arkona

This huge lake is a wonder rarely seen by travellers from the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md), but it supports many small communities who fish from its shores (and others who live beneath its surface). There are sharks in the waters, so do be aware.

Traversing the massive lake between [Tayama](Tayama.md) and [Blackstone](Blackstone.md) takes 2-3 days of continuous sailing on a [keelboat](keelboat.md).  A fully-crewed longship can usually do it in a little more than half that time.
