# Grimtooth Mountains (The Grimteeth)
Five narrow mountain peaks shaped like the teeth of a vast buried titan give the mountain range its name. [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) is the largest settlement in the mountains which are largely unsettled woods and craggy valleys, though there is a dirt road up to [Lake Arkona](LakeArkona.md) (through [Hammerford](Hammerford.md) and [Blackstone](Blackstone.md)). The [Dwarf Road](DwarfRoad.md) leads south out of the range to [Ikizh](Ikizh.md).
