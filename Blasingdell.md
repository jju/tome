# Blasingdell

A mining town in the [Grimteeth](Grimteeth.md) with a population of about 2000 diverse people. 

## Locations

* Tolm's Superior Outfitting and Dry Goods - run by Kheldegan Tolm ([Dwarf](Dwarf.md) [commoner](commoner.md))
* Griffon's Nest - inn and tavern owned by Sarel Bankdown ([Kenku](Kenku.md) [commoner](commoner.md))
* Temple of [Erathis](Erathis.md) - run by Sister Alonsa ([Halfling](Halfling.md) [Priest Acolyte](priest.md#Priest%20Acolyte))
* Watch house - Sergeant Grendar Kuln ([Orc](Orc.md) [mercenaryveteran](mercenaryveteran.md)) is in charge and constable Dara Whitewood ([Goliath](Goliath.md) [mercenaryveteran](mercenaryveteran.md)) is the senior officer. Watch has two more [guard](guard.md) and the militia could rouse about a hundred [commoners](commoner.md) for emergencies
* Town hall - Mayor Berrick Miles [Dwarf](Dwarf.md) [noble](noble.md)
