# Temple of Bahamut

Temple of [Bahamut](Bahamut.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) is a white stone building that has a number of kids in grey robes keeping it clean at all hours. The temple is led by [Brecken Dirtbone](BreckenDirtbone.md) (a gnomish [priest](rules/priest.md) with minor clerical abilities), who is usually too busy politicking to cast spells since this is one of the most official outposts of [Empire](DantagEmpire.md) in town.
