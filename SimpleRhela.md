# Simple Rhela - Priest of Gruumsh

Rhela is a dour [Orc](Orc.md) [monk](martialartsadept.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). Much of her work seems to be sitting and meditating as travellers pass through looking for her blessing. She rarely does much for these travellers beyond maintaining the rough-hewn temple (which has a lot of space for such a minimal practice).
