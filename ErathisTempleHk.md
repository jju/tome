# Temple of Erathis

[Hammerkeep's](Hammerkeep.md) temple of [Erathis](Erathis.md) is gray, stone and square, and built up on the hill, closer to the Keep than any of the others. [Dalrig Gravelmantle](DalrigGravelmantle.md), the [Dwarf](Dwarf.md) in charge, has a [military background](guard.md#Guard%20Captain) and openly disdains the [Warden of Hammerkeep](WardenofHammerkeep.md). When Paladins come to town there is a lot of politicking between the Erathis and [Bahamut temples](BahamutTempleHk.md).
