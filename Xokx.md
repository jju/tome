# Xokx

Xokx is a [harengon](harengon.md) blacksmith in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). She has gray-brown and white fur and stands about 4 1/2 feet tall (5' including ears). She has a bunch of bald patches on her ears and forearms from getting singed working on hot metal.

Xokx has a running feud with [Thalane Truthhauler Kalukithino](ThalaneTruthhaulerKalukithino.md), the [Goliath](Goliath.md) blacksmith two doors down from her. Xokx does her best to undercut Truthhauler's prices by pointing out that she makes useful things for small folk (he counters that you get much more for your silver if you buy from him). Xokx is also trying to get [Wenser Waterskin](WenserWaterskin.md) to abandon the old Goliath to come work for her and actually get good at their craft.
