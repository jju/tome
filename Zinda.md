# Zinda

Cultivate the Jeli flower. No standing army.

Languages: Common, N'warian, Draconic

Court of Flowers: heart of commerce and art district. Performers gather here and the guilds have towers.

Covenant of Magic: elderly traditional mages

Elucidarium: Bardic college, training for ruthless social climbers

Jeli Gardens: great gardens that can only grow here. The jungle just beyond is inhospitable.
