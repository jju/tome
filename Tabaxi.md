# Tabaxi

The Imperial sages describe Tabaxi -- the species blending humanoid and cats -- as follows:

> Some tabaxi have features or patterning in their fur like tigers, jaguars, or other big cats, while others have appearances more like a house cat. Still others have unique patterns or might style their fur to their preferences-or might even be hairless!

Tabaxi are common throughout the world.
