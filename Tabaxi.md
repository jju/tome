# Tabaxi

The Imperial sages describe Tabaxi -- the catlike humanoid species -- as follows:

> Some tabaxi have features or patterning in their fur like tigers, jaguars, or other big cats, while others have appearances more like a house cat. Still others have unique patterns or might style their fur to their preferences-or might even be hairless!

Tabaxi are common throughout the world, though in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) their settlements tend to be slightly rarer than others. In any of the major cities you'll find some. As far as my research has gone they seem to fill roles in the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md) commensurate to their population numbers, so no snarky comments on power here.
