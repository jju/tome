# Umizu
City-state with terrible yearly monsoon. Matrilineal society with formal duelling. Respect for ancestor spirits great and consultations with them are regular

Lacklustre bureaucracy and complacent samurai administrators makes for corruption. Crime syndicates  control underground trade.

Southwest Whaling Concern - merchant organization with private navy

Rurapo - clan of tritons who monitor overfishing and are deciding whether to wage war

Bright Moon Pier - marketplace and criminal syndicate the Safe Oceans Society controls gambling and smuggling with a blind eye turned by administrators

Umizu pickles are quite the delicacy

Governor's Palace - residence of mayor Daimyo Hogishi Takemi. Famous for elegant parties and annual poetry festival
