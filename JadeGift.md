# Jade Gift

Nonbinary [Tabaxi](Tabaxi.md) [Cleric](Cleric.md) of [Ioun](Ioun.md) at the [Circle of Healing Arts](CircleofHealingArts.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). They're the best spellcasting healer in the city and is constantly overworked, especially with the [Sereno](Sereno.md) outbreak of [IC4996](IC4996.md).
