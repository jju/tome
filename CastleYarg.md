# Castle Yarg

A lonely castle in [Cymir Yarg](CymirYarg.md) in the [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md). It was once known for its library, now ruined.

The library contained some of the last-known copies of the works of [Theopidus](Theopidus.md), [Kyser](Kyser.md), [Timius](Timius.md), [Errodius](Errodius.md), [Pun-Yeng](Pun-Yeng.md), [Muajid](Muajid.md) & [Yem](Yem.md) among more.
