# Grapplers' Creek

A small, primarily [Tiefling](Tiefling.md) village near [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) that focuses on cultivating purple apples and wild grapes on the banks of a tributary to the [NuoroRiver](NuoroRiver.md). According to a gnome wine wholesaler in Hammerkeep it has a "Faceless Stranger Approved" Bustle Factor of 6.2.

The village has a small gathering place in its temple of [Pelor](Pelor.md).

In the early spring/late winter of [IC4996](IC4996.md) the inhabitants of the town were almost all murdered by invisible clawed skulking beasts.
