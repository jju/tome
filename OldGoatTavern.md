# The Old Goat

A scummy drinking hole in the docks area of [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). It's owned by [VorkaThousandFists](VorkaThousandFists.md), who is a massive HalfOrc, who brooks almost no nonsense.

[JPRatquiat](JPRatquiat.md) is often sprawled here in a drug-fuelled haze. His dealer is [Tiefling](Tiefling.md) [Gluttony Jezebeth](GluttonyJezebeth.md)
