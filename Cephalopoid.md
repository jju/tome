# Tentacle-Faced Humanoids

In [IC4996](IC4996.md) [the party](TheParty.md) discovered [a small shrine](ShadowMirrorShrine.md) below some [standing stones](SkulkStones.md) in the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) near [Grapplers' Creek](GrapplersCreek.md). Inside the shrine they found statues of tentacle-faced humanoids lifting their hands toward the ceiling.
