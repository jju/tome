# Tentacle-Faced Humanoids

In [IC4996](IC4996.md) [the party](TheParty.md) discovered [a small shrine](ShadowMirrorShrine.md) below some [standing stones](SkulkStones.md) in the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) near [Grapplers' Creek](GrapplersCreek.md). Inside the shrine they found statues of tentacle-faced humanoids lifting their hands toward the ceiling.

[Pog](Pog.md) recognized these creatures as the characters in a gnomish epic wherein a kraken was flung to the top of a mountain and rescued by gnomes who shrunk the titan to manageable size, but the miniature kraken then divided and attached themselves to the gnomes' faces, controlling their bodies. Hilarity ensued. There are many volumes of these tales, colloquially known as the Tentacle Faced Babysitters Club saga.
