# Tentacle-Faced Humanoids

In [IC4996](IC4996.md) [the party](TheParty.md) discovered [a small shrine](ShadowMirrorShrine.md) below some [standing stones](WyrmstoneCircle.md) in the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) near [Grapplers' Creek](GrapplersCreek.md). Inside the shrine they found statues of tentacle-faced humanoids lifting their hands toward the ceiling.

[Pog](Pog.md) recognized these creatures as the characters in a gnomish epic. There are many volumes of these tales, colloquially known as the [Tentacle Faced Babysitters Club Saga](TentacleFacedBabysittersClubSaga.md).
