# The Twin Woods
When you need to refer to both the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) (north side of the [NuoroRiver](NuoroRiver.md)) and the [Karanliwood](Karanliwood.md) (south side) you can call them the Twin Woods **if** you're a snooty sage from the [Empire](DantagEmpire.md). Anyone local is either specific about them, or calls them the Woods (since there's no doubt which woods you'd be discussing).
