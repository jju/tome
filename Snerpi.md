# Snerpi - Priest of Melora
A small round [Kobold](Kobold.md) [commoner](commoner.md) priest of [Pelor](Pelor.md) who loves telling jokes and is legitimately the nicest, kindest person in the city of [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). He generally stays out of politics and just wants to see people well-fed and happy.
