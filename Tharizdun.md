# Tharizdun

The Chained Oblivion is chaos and darkness unending. Its cultists work without word towards the Epoch of Ends where it will be unleashed and all beings are consumed.

Usually depicted as a spreading cloud of lightless destruction (sometimes a spiral in a seven-pointed star).
#### Commandments

* Offer and siphon power to Tharizdun until it is liberated.
* Uncover, restore and exalt forgotten shrines and relics in its honour.
* Ruin and raze the realms to prepare for the Epoch of Ends.
