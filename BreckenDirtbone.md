# Brecken Dirtbone - Bahamut Priest
[Gnomish](Gnome.md) [Priest](rules/priest.md#Priest) of [Bahamut](Bahamut.md) in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). They're a political animal, always too busy to be casting mere healing spells (though they're one of like 5 people in the city who can do it). Brecken **hates** [Dalrig Gravelmantle](DalrigGravelmantle.md) and is constantly trying to maintain any advantage they can over the temple of [Erathis](Erathis.md).
