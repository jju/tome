## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The giant makes two attacks, using Frost Axe or Great Bow in any combination.

_**Frost Axe.** Melee Attack Roll:_ +9, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 19 (2d12 + 6) Slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) Cold damage.

_**Great Bow.** Ranged Attack Roll:_ +9, range 150/600 ft. _Hit:_ 17 (2d10 + 6) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Cold damage, and the target’s Speed decreases by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

## Bonus Actions

_**War Cry (Recharge 5–6).**_ The giant or one creature of its choice that can see or hear it gains 16 (2d10 + 5) [Temporary Hit Points](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#TemporaryHitPoints) and has [Advantage](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Advantage) on attack rolls until the start of the giant’s next turn.
