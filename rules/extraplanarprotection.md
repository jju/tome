# Protection from Evil and Good

_Concentration_

**Level** 1st
**Casting Time** 1 Action
**Range/Area** Touch
**Components** V, S, M (a flask of Holy Water worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)
**Duration** _Concentration_ 10 Minutes
**School** Abjuration
**Classes** [Cleric](Cleric.md) [Druid](Druid.md) [Paladin](Paladin.md) [Warlock](Warlock.md) [Wizard](Wizard.md)

**Attack/Save** None
**Damage/Effect** Buff (...)

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch is protected against creatures that are Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, or Undead. The protection grants several benefits. Creatures of those types have Disadvantage on attack rolls against the target. The target also can’t be [possessed](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Possession) by or gain the [Charmed](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#CharmedCondition) or [Frightened](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#FrightenedCondition) conditions from them. If the target is already possessed, Charmed, or Frightened by such a creature, the target has Advantage on any new saving throw against the relevant effect.
