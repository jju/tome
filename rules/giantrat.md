# Traits

_**Pack Tactics.**_ The rat has [Advantage](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Advantage) on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the rat’s allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn’t have the [Incapacitated](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#IncapacitatedCondition) condition.
