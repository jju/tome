Rat folk are humanoid rats. They have rat-like heads, with large ears, long snouts, whiskers, and large, strong teeth. Their bodies are covered in short fur but are otherwise human in form. 

## Ratfolk Traits

**Creature Type:** [Humanoid](Humanoid.md)
**Size:** Small or Medium
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Age.** Ratfolks reach adulthood at 12 and can live up to 50 years.

**Brethren of the Swarm.** Mice and rats of all types will not show unprovoked hostility towards you unless threatened. Additionally, you can communicate simple ideas with them through speaking Rattus.

**Keen Senses.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that involve hearing or smell.
**Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Rattus. Rattus is a dialect of Sylvan which is spoken with an inflection that favors chitters and squeaks.
**Scurry.** You are able to slip and squeeze through small openings. You have advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) to fit through tight spaces and squeezing through narrow passages doesn't cost you extra movement.

**Age**. Rat folk reach maturity more quickly than humans, becoming adults after ten years. They live to be about sixty.

#### Iron Stomach (option)
Perhaps as a feature of your rat heritage, you can eat virtually anything without concern. You have advantage on saving throws against poison, and you have resistance against poison damage.

#### Rat Folk Appraisal (option)
Rat folk communities often support themselves by collecting finding things of value from amidst the cast offs and garbage of other communities. You have proficiency in the Survival skill. When you are searching in an urban area for useful things or things of value, you have advantage on the check.

## Fancy Rats

Among the mischiefs which settle into the sleepy corners of cities are those Ratfolk who busy themselves with past times and small talk. 

**Languages.** In addition to Common and Rattus, you know two additional languages of your choice.
**Affable Candor.** You gain proficiency in the Persuasion skill.
**Curious Mind.** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.

## Pack Rats

Ratfolk who seem content to set up a shop of curiosities, a small work bench, or an eclectic haberdashery and earn their coin with their hands. These are the Pack Rats.

**Appraiser's Eye.** You have advantage on Intelligence (Arcana and Investigation) checks to identify objects and their worth.
**Crafty Hands.** You gain one tool proficiency of your choice.
**Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

**Natural Swimmer.** You have a swim speed 30 feet and can hold your breath for 15 minutes.
**Hardy Constitution.** You have advantage on saving throws against diseases and poison.
**Tunnel Crawler.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to navigate while underground.

### Water Rats
Ratfolk who make their living by the waters of the forest or upon the high seas have the following Ability (instead of Tunnel Crawler).

**River Runner.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to navigate while on or by lakes, ponds, rivers, or shorelines.

## Wood Rat

They strive for a simple life consisting of communal farming, sustainable foraging, and the occasional wanderings through their neck of the woods.

**Forest Touch.** You know the druidcraft cantrip.
**Stealthy Journeys.** You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, falling snow, mist, and other natural phenomena.
**Woodland Tracking.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to find and gather food, and have advantage on Intelligence (Nature) checks to identify and recall information about beasts, monstrosities, and plants.
