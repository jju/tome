## Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The bandit makes two attacks, using Scimitar and Pistol in any combination.

_**Scimitar.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage.

_**Pistol.**_ _Ranged Attack Roll:_ +5, range 30/90 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage.

## Reactions

_**Parry.**_ _Trigger:_ The bandit is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon. _Response:_ The bandit adds 2 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
