### Invested Protection

1st-level evocation

**Casting Time**: 1 action
**Range**: Touch
**Components**: V, S
**Duration**: Instantaneous and 1 minute
**Class(es)**: [cleric](https://www.5esrd.com/classes/cleric), [paladin](https://www.5esrd.com/classes/paladin)

You infuse a living creature with healing energies, restoring 1d6 [hit points](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/combat#TOC-Hit-Points) immediately. In addition, for 1 minute these energies surround the target and cause all attacks against them to take a -1 penalty on [damage rolls](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/combat#TOC-Damage-Rolls).

An undead creature striking the target in melee with a part of their body takes 1 radiant damage after each hit. Undead wielding a weapon do not take this damage.

**At Higher Levels**. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you restore an additional 1d6 [hit points](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/combat#TOC-Hit-Points) to the target and increase the penalty on [damage rolls](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/combat#TOC-Damage-Rolls) against the target and radiant damage taken by undead striking the target in melee by 1.
