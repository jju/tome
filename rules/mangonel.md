A Mangonel is a catapult that hurls heavy projectiles in a high arc, so it can hit targets behind walls.

Mangonel 

Large Object 
Armor Class: 15 
Hit Points: 100 

Loading a Mangonel requires two ***Utilize*** actions, and aiming it requires two more ***Utilize*** actions. Then a crew member can take the Mangonel Stone action.

***Mangonel Stone (Requires Load and Aim).** Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, range 200/800 ft. (can’t hit targets within 60 feet of itself). *Hit:* 27 (5d10) Bludgeoning damage.
