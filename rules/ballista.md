A Ballista is a massive crossbow that fires heavy bolts.

Large Object 
AC: 15 
HP: 50 

Loading a Ballista requires the ***Utilize*** action, and aiming it requires another ***Utilize*** action. Then a crew member can take the Ballista Bolt action.

***Ballista Bolt (Requires Load and Aim).** Ranged Attack Roll:* +6, range 120/480 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d10) Piercing damage.
