
## Combat Turns 
use D&D Combat rules from the [SRD](srd).

## Dungeon Turns

roll on [dungeon](dungeon.md) for every 10 minutes exploring a room

## Wilderness Turns
roll on [wilderness](wilderness.md) for every 4 hours exploring a hex

## Travel Turns
roll on for every day of travelling

## Downtime Turns
roll on [downtime](downtime.md) to see how long the turn lasts
