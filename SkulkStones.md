# Standing Stones Clearing

A few miles into the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) from [Grapplers' Creek](GrapplersCreek.md) there are 4 standing stones surrounding a round flat one in a clearing.
## History

According to [Ned Lolvodug](NedLolvodug.md) (noted non-historian):

> Long ago terrible things were going on and horror erupted out of the ground. Somehow they magically sealed it up the rupture using these stones. To protect the stones from jackasses undoing all their work, a super-powerful druid created a guardian to protect it. [The Guardian](BranchGolem.md) would live forever and protect the stones as long as the pact between the woods and the plains held. 
> 
> The pact was sealed by gifting the Guardian a person and an apple covered in honey by the final new moon of each year. The [Queen of the Woods](QueenoftheWoods.md) is responsible for the honey-covered apple and the [Warden of Hammerkeep](WardenofHammerkeep.md) is responsible for the person. Seems like a lopsided deal, but [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) usually had extra prisoners hanging around. 

## Current Events

[The Party](TheParty) [tracked](Hk01-InvestigationInvisible.md) some [invisible beasts](Skulk.md) to these stones in [IC4996](IC4996.md) and discovered one of the stones had been partially toppled. Beneath the toppled stone was a passageway with descending stairs. The central stone was also making loud booming noises.

On their [second encounter](Hk02-MessMirrors.md) they discovered there may be a pattern to the booming noises from beneath. They also rolled the toppled stone out from its spot blocking the descending passageway.

In the clearing around the stones, [Barfgeld](Barfgeld.md) found a weird bunch of wood hanging from a tree, which reminded him of the warding bundles his grandmother used to hang in trees to keep evil spirits away.

When [Pog](Pog.md) arrived [for the second time](Hk03-MurderCurtains.md) he did some experimental drumming to see if the booming noises echoed or followed some other pattern. It appeared to be a different number of booms depending on which stone was touched.

Later on, the [Lolvodug Patrol](LolPatrol.md) arrived looking for this year's bearers of the honey-covered apple, who never returned. Once [Pog](Pog.md) descended, they investigated the clearing, finding the weird bunch of wood hanging in the tree and identifying it as the stone's [Guardian](BranchGolem.md) that had somehow been killed.

As of Iall 5, 4996 his party is heading back to [Padserae](Padserae.md) to inform the [Queen of the Woods](QueenoftheWoods.md) that the Guardian is dead and possibly the protective pact with [Rundan](Rundan.md) is broken.
