# Seree

Suppressed in the Imperial histories are the records of the Seree, who made an attempt to organize and control [Nuoro](Nuoro.md) in sorcerous resistance after the [Arcane Purge](ArcanePurge.md). Some say the Seree were their own empire, brought down by demigods themselves (controversially demigods unconnected to the official [Dantag Imperial](DantagEmpire.md) religions.
