# Stouthearts

A somewhat common term for [Halflings](Halfling.md) in the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md), especially those who are native to [Nuoro](Nuoro.md). Usually used as opposed to [Hairfeet](Hairfeet.md) or [Tallfellows](Tallfellows.md), which is used more in [Anor](Anor.md).
