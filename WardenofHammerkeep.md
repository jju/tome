# Suffering Yilmaz - Warden of Hammerkeep

Yilmaz is a large curly-horned [Tiefling](Tiefling.md) [knight](knight.md) based in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). Ostensibly he's in charge of organizing the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md) patrols to keep passage upriver safe for the [Dantag Empire](DantagEmpire.md). He's not especially diligent at the task.

His lieutenant is named [Ofuzz](Ofuzz.md) and she is the one who's seen more around town (and leading whatever patrols do go out).
