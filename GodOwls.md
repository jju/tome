# Weird Owl Beast

Near the end of [IC4995](IC4995.md) a strange beast began flying around near [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). Untrustworthy witnesses said it looked like a huge owl that was shedding feathers. [Rurik Stoneblood](Rurik.md) is pretty certain it's some sort of godly beast and has tried making effigies of it in metal.
