A cult dedicated to bringing the dragon god [Tiamat](Tiamat.md) to the world through celebrating greed and self-interest.

Their symbol is a dragon claw emerging from a demicircle.

## Characters

* [FrulamMondath](FrulamMondath.md) - local cult leader in the [Grimteeth](Grimteeth.md)
* Langderosa Cyanwrath - blue halfdragon with big sense of honour when it comes to one on one combat, Mondath's assistant
* Rezmir - black halfdragon (female)
* AzbaraJos - wizard
