# Cyzzarun

The great [Gold Dragon](golddragon.md#Adult%20Gold%20Dragon), symbol of [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md). While the banner bearing its symbol is constantly flying over the city, the dragon itself only makes rare appearances.

Everyone knows[^1] that Cyzzarun turned the tide of the [Grim War](GrimWar.md), and was the main reason the Dwarves gave up their foolish evil war in the face of [Bahamut's](Bahamut.md) chosen[^2] descending upon them.
