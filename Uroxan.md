# Uroxan - Town Guard

[Dragonborn](Dragonborn.md) town [guard](guard.md) in [Dennel Crossing](DennelCrossing). Along with [Ifa](Ifa.md), he was beaten up by [The Party](TheParty.md) on [Iall 25, 4996](Hk05-MurderHobosHoles.md). He had previously been out to the old Rat King mine to make sure it was still sealed up when the [Sereno](Sereno.md) outbreak began.
