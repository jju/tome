Green is a male-presenting [Halfling](Halfling.md) with light skin and a bit of a snarl. He carries a dagger, but anyone who meets him can usually tell quickly he's a lot more talky than stabby.

He and his companion [Urdl](Urdl.md) were attacked by [Kobolds](Kobold.md) on the eastern road to [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) and were picked up by [Rexer III](RexerIII.md) and [Fillika](Fillika.md) afterwards[^1].

Green is of the opinion that he and [Urdl](Urdl.md) are in big trouble with their boss for getting ambushed. He also thinks this is all the boss' fault and they should have paid for someone from one of the [GoblinTransportGuilds](GoblinTransportGuilds.md) to ensure this kind of thing didn't happen. He isn't super forthcoming about them trying to catch up with the wagon of cargo they're supposed to be protecting on its way to [Phandalin](Phandalin.md) but they weren't protecting it because he thought this route through the woods would let them catch up with it after sleeping late. The wagon driver is an old [Tabaxi](Tabaxi.md) [commoner](commoner.md) named Frum. His goal is to arrive in [Phandalin](Phandalin.md) with the wagon of goods and their boss [GundrenRockseeker](GundrenRockseeker.md) none the wiser.
