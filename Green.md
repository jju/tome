Green is a male-presenting [Halfling](Halfling.md) with light skin and a bit of a snarl. He carries a dagger, but anyone who meets him can usually tell quickly he's a lot more talky than stabby.

He and his companion [Urdl](Urdl.md) were attacked by [Kobolds](Kobold.md) on the eastern road to [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) and were picked up by [Rexer III](RexerIII.md) and [Fillika](Fillika.md) afterwards[^1].

Green is of the opinion that he and [Urdl](Urdl.md) are in big trouble with their boss for getting ambushed and losing their cargo. He also thinks this is all the boss' fault and they should have paid for someone from one of the [GoblinTransportGuilds](GoblinTransportGuilds.md) to ensure this kind of thing didn't happen.
