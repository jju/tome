# Orfuzz - Assistant to the Warden of Hammerkeep

Ofuzz is a small female presenting [Goblin](Goblin.md) [Warrior](rules/goblin.md#Goblin%20Boss) who works for [Suffering Yilmaz](WardenofHammerkeep.md). She has long dark hair and wears roughly beaded rocks that may or may not be valuable stones around her neck and braided in her (usually pretty tangled) hair. She can come on a little strong to outsiders.

Orfuzz has tried to pocket a cool 20% of the fee [The Party](TheParty.md) was supposed to be getting.
