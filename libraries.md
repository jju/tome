# Libraries of Nuoro

Apparently the best library in Nuoro for any sort of arcane knowledge was in Cymir Yarg, somewhere in the Karanliwood but that's more of a rumour, since no one has been there in centuries, but they say it survived the Purge.

The Temple of Ioun in Bedwistre could have more information on magickal artifacts but would alert authorities you're looking into this. 

The Hammerkeep Library won't have anything about these sorts of things. 

Raei Temple in the Copper Hills did have at least a small library, but it's been abandoned for decades.
