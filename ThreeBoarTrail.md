The Three Boar Trail descends from the [Grimteeth](Grimteeth.md) into the [Kanliwood](Kanliwood.md). It goes through [Conyberry?](Conyberry.md), with other trails branching out along the way.

This is the main route from [Dracolathe](Dracolathe.md) to [Rundan](Rundan.md) so you often will see carts and wagons groaning their way along. In the Grimtooth section the trail can get washed out in storms, but there are relatively few miles where rockslides are a huge concern.
