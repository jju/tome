# Vecna, the Whispered One

The Lich Lord was once an archmage who cheated death and usurped power to become a god themself. They wander the planes looking for powerful artifacts, ruling over that which was not meant to be known.

Symbolized by their hand and eye which were left behind when they were dissolved and reconstructed.

They loathe the other gods, with an especial hatred for [Ioun](Ioun.md). The [Raven Queen](RavenQueen.md) hates Vecna for affronting her purpose.
#### Commandments

* Learn all you can and keep hidden what you know.
* Express and cultivate evil in yourself and recognize it in others so as to exploit them.
* Seed the ruin of those who worship other deities so that someday all bow before Vecna.
