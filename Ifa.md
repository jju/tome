# Ifa - Town Guard

[Aarakocra](Aarakocra.md) town [guard](guard.md) in [Dennel Crossing](DennelCrossing). Along with [Uroxan](Uroxan.md), she was beaten up (and magically healed) by [The Party](TheParty.md) on [Iall 25, 4996](Hk05-MurderHobosHoles.md). She had previously been out to the old Rat King mine to make sure it was still sealed up when the [Sereno](Sereno.md) outbreak began. She has a sister named [Agla](Agla.md).
