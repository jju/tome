# Shanj

Shanj is a strategic boardgame growing in popularity in [Nuoro](Nuoro.md). Sometimes known as dragonchess.

It's seen as a means of expression among its practitioners, and some take it very seriously. In [Ikizh](Ikizh.md) it's very common in civilized and not-so- communities for insults and disagreements to be resolved over the board for forfeits.

[GwarglKelHuun](GwarglKelHuun.md) heard of a tournament happening in [Rundan](Rundan.md) in [summer of IC4996](IC4996.md) and was heading there.
