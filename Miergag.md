# Miergag

The legendary [Ancient Red Dragon](reddragon.md#Ancient%20Red%20Dragon), slain by the [Dwarves](Dwarf.md) in ages long past. Miergag's hoard fuelled wealth in the [Grimteeth](Grimteeth.md) for generations, and the city of [Zhaalum](Zhaalum.md) was built on its site.
