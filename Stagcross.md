The midpoint along the Tozlu road between [Khudurra](Khudurra.md) and [Sivekkah](Sivekkah.md) this crossroads village (pop 484) is most notable for its stables and fast horses. It's about 60 miles to each city.

Stagcross has a gold limit of 200 gold pieces. No items are for sale that cost more than this amount.

Community Authorities

Seaton has 4 full time guards, which is small for this size community. In an emergency, 103 adults can be conscripted, which is large for this size community.

There is no official guard captain in Seaton. The guard reports to Chairman Mary Cornish.
