# Blighted Order of Sorg

A cult worshipping [Torog](Torog.md) (or at least the version of Torog called [Sorg](Sorg.md)). They've holed up in the old [Temple of Raei](RaeiTempleCH.md) in the [Copper Hills](CopperHills.md) over the winter. In [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md) it's thought this was a bandit gang that had been operating in the area last year.
