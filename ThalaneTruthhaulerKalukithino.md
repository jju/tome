# Thalane Truthhauler Kalukithino

Truthhauler is a half-blind old [Goliath](Goliath.md) blacksmith in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). He has a running feud with [[Xokx]], the blacksmith two doors down from his shop. His apprentice, [WenserWaterskin](WenserWaterskin.md) is a shy impressionable child who can't do anything right.

He makes good strong farming equipment most of the time, but can do weapons and armour if pressed. His main problem is making things small enough for non-Goliaths to use. [Xokx](Xokx.md) does not hesitate to take advantage of that weakness and often pitches sales to people about to make Truthhauler purchases.
