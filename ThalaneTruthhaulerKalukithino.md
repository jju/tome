# Thalane Truthhauler Kalukithino

Truthhauler is a [Goliath](Goliath.md) blacksmith in [Hammerkeep](Hammerkeep.md). He has a running feud with [[Xokx]] the blacksmith two doors down from his shop. His apprentice, [WenserWaterskin](WenserWaterskin.md) is a shy impressionable halfling.
